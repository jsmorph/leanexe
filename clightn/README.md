# Reference semantics N

N is a reference semantics for the Clight forms that the C translator emits, written once in a neutral language in [the specification](N.nsx) and rendered mechanically into Rocq and Lean.  [The check](check.sh) confirms from what each prover elaborated that the two renderings say what the specification says, so the step between the provers rests on a mechanical comparison in place of the review of a hand transcription.  N covers the leaf forms, which each prover proves of its own definitions: the operators at the type pairs that the translator emits on integers and on `double` and `float` numbers, the unary operators on floating-point numbers, the casts, the truth values of conditions, and the constants.  The leaf forms on floating-point numbers give their results through [the floating-point equations](#the-floating-point-equations): each names an operation by CompCert's definition in Rocq and by the Lean copy's in Lean, and N assumes that the two agree, which the check lists as an assumption.  N also runs expressions, statements, calls, and whole programs over temporaries, scalar globals, and the bytes of arrays such as `lx_mem` and `lx_pun`, with the cells of `lx_pun` at every type that the translator gives them, the built-in square root, the volatile accesses of the bare-metal `main` and their events, and it states the judgment of a `main` that runs forever.  Rocq proves that every derivation of N is a derivation of CompCert 3.18's `Clight2` semantics, and Lean proves that every execution of the Lean copy of Clight is a derivation of N for the programs that the translator's forms make up.  For the whole programs of [the table of instances](#the-programs), the energy controller's gateway and the Euler solver among them, the chain runs from the copy's theorem in Lean to the premises of CompCert's theorems for AArch64 in Rocq: `clight2_bigstep_to_asm` for a terminating bare-metal program, `clight2_bigstep_asm_reads_ptr64` for a program with input, and `clight2_loop_to_asm_ptr64` for a program whose `main` runs forever.  Between the provers the chain rests on the check and the floating-point equations, and each Rocq theorem also assumes that `clightgen`'s exporter printed the program that `clightgen` holds, as [the section on the programs](#the-programs) states.  [The translator design](../docs/native-translator.md#trust-argument-and-validation) describes what N carries and what remains under review and tests.

## The neutral language

A specification is a sequence of S-expressions.  The symbol table declares each sort, data constructor, base symbol, and interpreted symbol with its name in Rocq and its name in Lean, and the renderer writes those names while the check expects them in the elaborated terms.  A name may be an application whose arguments are fixed: the types and instance of the addition of integers, or the type arguments of a polymorphic type or constructor such as `Gvar`, so that the check confirms the arguments that elaboration chose.  Data constructors are the syntax and values that both provers share by name: types, operators, comparisons, expressions, statements, functions, global definitions, initialization data, external functions, outcomes, events, values, and the natural numbers `O` and `S`.  The sort `ident` is `positive` in Rocq and `Nat` in Lean, whose 0 no program uses, the sorts `float` and `float32` are the binary64 and binary32 numbers of each prover, and the sort `Prop` gives the type of an invariant.  Interpreted symbols name the definitions under test, which the twin files `NBase.v` and `Base.lean` map to CompCert's `sem_binary_operation`, `sem_unary_operation`, `sem_cast`, `bool_val`, and `eval_expr` and to the Lean copy's at `Archi.aarch64`.  An equation, `(equation NAME SORT (rocq R) (lean L))`, is a symbol of the sort `SORT` whose meaning is the constant `R` in Rocq and the constant `L` in Lean, which N assumes to agree on every argument.  Each rendering defines `eqn_NAME` of the sort `SORT` as the equation's constant, so that the check confirms both constants and the sort.

A relation is a list of constructors, each a closed formula whose conclusion applies the relation, and a statement is a closed formula.  Formulas use `forall`, `exists`, implication, conjunction, disjunction, equivalence, negation, equality, `Some`, `None`, the lists `cons` and `nil`, the pairs `pair`, natural numerals, numerals of identifiers `(lit ident N)`, strings of letters, digits, and underscores, and applications of symbols, relations, and bound variables.  Sorts are the declared ones and their options, lists, products, and functions.  The language has no function definitions, pattern matching, or conditional terms, so that every declaration elaborates to a formula that a small reader prints and compares.  A macro is expanded before rendering and before comparison, and an expansion that would capture a variable of its arguments is an error.  A further specification file starts with `(part (rocq MODULE))` and adds constants, `(constant NAME SORT VALUE)`, and statements to N, rendered into the Rocq module `MODULE` and a Lean module of the namespace `ClightN.Spec`.  A statement declared with `(carried NAME (rocq THEOREM) FORMULA)` is proved in Lean and is the first premise of the Rocq theorem `THEOREM`.

The base symbols are integer arithmetic and order, the value of a word or pointer offset (`u32`, `u64`, `uptr`) and the word or offset of an integer (`r32`, `r64`, `rptr`), the signed value of a 64-bit word (`s64`), the quotient and remainder by a positive divisor (`zdiv`, `zmod`), the power of two of a number that is not negative (`pow2`), the bitwise operations on numbers that are not negative (`land`, `lor`, `lxor`), the concatenation of traces (`app`), and the floating-point numbers of bit patterns and their bits (`f64_of_bits`, `f64_bits`, `f32_of_bits`, `f32_bits`).  The laws at the end of the specification characterize every base symbol other than arithmetic and order on the arguments with which N uses it, and both provers prove them.  The meaning that the check trusts is therefore integer arithmetic and order, the identification of words by their values, the names of the definitions under test, and the floating-point equations.

## The relations of N

N runs a program over three maps: the temporaries of the running function and the scalar globals, from identifiers to values, and the bytes of the arrays, from identifiers and offsets to numbers.  The scalar globals are the writable, non-volatile variables of type `unsigned int`, `unsigned long long`, `double`, or `float`, the last two initialized by the space of their bytes, so that each holds at the start the number whose bits are 0, and an array is a writable, non-volatile variable of `n` words of type `unsigned long long` initialized by one `Init_space` of its `8 n` bytes, such as `lx_mem` and `lx_pun`.  N's relations take the program's definitions as a parameter, among which a call finds its function and an access its variable.  `neval` evaluates constants, temporaries, scalar globals, the leaf operators, unary operators, and casts, and the three shapes in which the translator reads an array: the word at an index, `*(x + i)`, the byte at an index, `((unsigned char *) x)[i]`, and the cell of type `unsigned long long`, `unsigned int`, `double`, or `float` at offset 0, `*(ty *) x`.  A read requires its bytes to lie within the array, at the offset that CompCert's pointer arithmetic computes, and gives the little-endian number of the bytes, or, for a cell of type `double` or `float`, the floating-point number of that number's bits.  `has_type` gives the type of each such expression.

`nexec` follows `exec_stmt` of `ClightBigstep.v` with `function_entry2`: `Sskip`, `Sset`, the stores to a scalar global and to a word, a byte, or a cell of an array, `Ssequence`, `Sifthenelse`, `Sloop` with its three rules, `Sbreak`, `Scontinue`, `Sreturn`, a call of an internal function by its name, whose rule executes the callee's body from its bound parameters and undefined temporaries, and a call of the built-in `__builtin_fsqrt`, which sets its temporary to the square root of its argument with no event.  A store to an array writes the bytes of the stored number from the lowest, and a store to a cell of type `double` or `float` the bytes of the number's bits.  The volatile accesses are the store of an `unsigned int` to a variable of type `volatile unsigned int`, the load of an element of a volatile array of `unsigned char` or `unsigned int` at an index, which gives its temporary the zero-extended value of any word that its event holds, and the store of an `unsigned int` to an element of a volatile array of `unsigned int`.  Each access is one event of the trace.  `ninit` states the initial state: distinct identifiers, initialization data that CompCert's `init_mem` accepts, the initial value of each scalar global, and zero in each byte of each array.  `nprog` runs a `main` of type `int (void)` from that state to an `int` result.  `nreacts` is the judgment of a `main` that runs forever, the twin of `LoopReacts` of the Lean copy and `loop_reacts` of `rocq/Clight2Asm.v`: `main` runs the statements before its loop with a trace `t0` and then iterations of the loop, iteration `n` with the nonempty trace `W n`, through an invariant over N's states that holds after the first statements and that each iteration preserves.  `nreads σ c t` states that the trace `t` holds only volatile loads and stores and that its `k`-th load reads `σ (c + k)`.

`nprog_sound` in `NExec.v` carries `nprog` of a program's definitions and `main` to `Clight2.bigstep_program_terminates` of the program, `nreacts_sound` carries `nreacts` to `loop_reacts`, and `nreads_reads` carries `nreads σ 0` to `reads σ`.  The proofs relate N's temporaries to CompCert's, N's scalar globals to the memory, where each holds its value at offset 0 with a writable permission, and N's bytes of an array to the contents of its block, which are writable within the array, and they build the `Clight2` derivation by induction on N's.  `program_to_n` in `Exec.lean` carries the copy's `bigstep_program_terminates` at `Archi.aarch64` to `nprog`, and `reacts_to_n` carries `LoopReacts` to `nreacts`, for a program that `progOk` accepts: its identifiers are distinct, its variables have the initialization data that N accepts, and the functions that `main` reaches use only the forms above.  The proof follows the copy's derivation through the mutual recursion of `exec_stmt` and `eval_funcall`.  It carries the invariants that no temporary holds a pointer, that each `return` gives a value of a leaf cast to the function's result type, and that an array's block has permissions only within its bytes, which `init_mem` gives and which stores keep, so that a load or store that succeeds lies within the array.  The copy writes the trace of a loop's three runs as `(t1 ++ t2) ++ t3` and CompCert as `t1 ** (t2 ** t3)`, and N follows CompCert, so the Lean proof applies the associativity of concatenation.

`emit.lean` writes the C text of each program as `native-bare` writes it, and the bytes of a bare-metal program's output. The language of N has no recursive definitions, so the statement names that trace by a function and its equations.  The scripts are untrusted, since each prover checks the constants that they write.

`Instance.lean` holds what the Lean proofs share.  An instance's whole program is `wholeOf post T fd`: the translation `T`, CompCert's declarations among the file's definitions `fd` with the whole program's identifiers, and the definitions `post` after the translation's, which differ between bare-metal programs, programs with input or controllers, and programs with requests.  `InstOk post T fd fm` states what the kernel checks: renaming the whole program by position gives the file's definitions and `main`, the renaming is injective on the program's globals and on each function's temporaries, CompCert's declarations satisfy `PrefixOk`, and `progOk` accepts the file's program with the functions that `reach` finds from `main` by calls.  The tactic `inst_ok` proves it by one kernel evaluation for each condition, in a declaration of its own, so that the kernel holds the terms of one condition at a time.  The renaming finds identifiers in binary tries on their bits, the tests of membership and repetition use the same tries, and `defsEq` compares the programs with the rest of a list or a sequence of statements compared last, so that the kernel's work grows with the occurrences of identifiers times their bits, and the depth of its recursion with the nesting of statements.  From `InstOk`, `terminates_n` carries a termination of the whole program through the renaming theorem and `program_to_n` to the file's program in N, `bare_n` carries the disjunction that `bare_program` states, given the trace of the file's identifiers, `reads_n` carries the terminations and reads of a program with input through `reads_nreads`, and `reacts_n` carries `LoopReacts` for every `σ` and the reads of every prefix of the windows through `loopReacts_rename`, `reacts_to_n`, and `reads_nreads`.  The renaming that `exists_injective_renaming` gives depends on the program alone, so `W` and `P` are functions of `σ` without a choice.  Each Lean file of `lean/` applies one of these to the copy's theorem: the reads of a controller's windows come from `ctl_reads` and those of a program with requests from `req_reads`, and the kernel computes a bare-metal program's output bytes, after `gcd_value` and `recursive_id` give the results of `gcd` and `recursive`, which recurse by well-founded recursion and which the kernel does not evaluate, and after `solve_four` gives the result of the Euler solver.

The kernel evaluates the Euler solver's arithmetic through the model of Lean's `Float`, and one evaluation of the whole run holds the terms of every step and exceeds 40 GB.  The command `euler_steps` of `lean/Euler.lean` runs the solver natively and adds, for each of its steps, the state after the step as words, the time's bits and each cell's quantities' bits, and the theorem that the step takes the state before it to the state after it, which the kernel checks by evaluation, each in a declaration of its own and one after another, so that the kernel holds one step's terms at a time.  The theorems state the loop's initial state, condition, and step as the equation of `runFromInto 4` holds them, and the theorems `run<k>` and `stop`, which follow the loop `repeatWhile` through them, state its fuel as the numeral that the equation holds.  The proof of `solve_four` rewrites under the matches of `solve` and `runFromInto`, and the kernel compares two applications of a matcher by unfolding them, which runs the loop unless their discriminants are the same term.  The proof therefore rewrites by the definitions' equations, where `unfold` would leave the kernel to compare a match on `repeatWhile …` with a match on `repeatWhile.go …`, and by theorems whose fuel is the goal's numeral.  `dec_enc` turns the words back into the state, and `solve_four` states the packed result of the last state.  The native run only proposes the states, since the kernel checks each step and each test of the loop's condition.

The definitions of the `depth` program include two functions of `Verified.Examples.Fold` on `double`, which its `main` does not call.  N's program judgment takes the whole list of definitions, and its call rule runs only a function that a call names, so `progOk` checks every variable of the program and the functions that `main` reaches.  No function is removed: the statement, and the Rocq theorem with it, describe the program that `clightgen` printed, with all of its definitions.  The step of the controller declares a temporary of type `double` or `float` for each local of that type in the step's WebAssembly function and initializes it with a constant.  N covers every form that the translator emits for the native corpus: `progOk`'s checks accept every function of the bare-metal programs and of the translations of `tests/verified/modules.txt`.

`NInst.v` holds what the Rocq theorems share.  `terminates_asm` carries `nprog` of a file's program through `nprog_sound`, `export_terminates`, and `clight2_bigstep_to_asm` to the Asm program's termination, `reads_asm` carries the terminations that read every `σ` through `nreads_reads` and `clight2_bigstep_asm_reads_ptr64`, so that each behavior of the Asm program is such a termination, and `reacts_asm` carries the reactive judgment through `nreacts_sound`, `export_reacts`, `prefix_windows`, which identifies `P σ` with `windows (W σ)` of `rocq/Clight2Asm.v`, and `clight2_loop_to_asm_ptr64`: for every `σ` the Asm program reacts with the infinite trace of the windows `W σ`, whose loads read `σ`, and each behavior of the Asm program is bisimilar to one of these.  Each instance's theorem takes as premises, besides the statement and the compilation of CompCert, that the exporter of `clightgen` printed the program inside `clightgen` as the file's definitions (`export_defs` of `NExport.v` transcribes `stmt` of `export/ExportClight.ml`), and that the two programs have the same `main`, public identifiers, and composite types.  As for every program of the native path, the chain also trusts that `clightgen` and `ccomp` run the same front end.  The Rocq theorems concern the program compiled for AArch64, and a build of the same C text for ARMv7-M lies outside them, since the proofs need `Archi.ptr64 = true`.  The statements leave out what N has no definition of: the result and output of the program with input, the SHA-256 digest of its message, and the stored bytes, the states, and the requests of a `main` that runs forever, which the copy's theorems give.

## The floating-point equations

N's leaf forms on floating-point numbers give their results through the operations of the table, each an `equation` of `N.nsx` whose meaning is CompCert 3.18's definition of `lib/Floats.v` in Rocq and the Lean copy's of `LeanExe/Native/Clight/Floats.lean` at `Archi.aarch64` in Lean.  N assumes that the two definitions of an equation agree on every argument, with the floating-point numbers identified across the provers by their bit patterns: the laws of `f64_of_bits` and `f64_bits`, and of `f32_of_bits` and `f32_bits`, make them inverse bijections in each prover.  No proof in either prover supports an equation, so the step between the provers rests on them.  The check confirms each equation's two constants and its sort from the definitions `eqn_NAME` that the renderings hold, `nsx.py check` ends with a line that names the equations as the assumptions of the step between the provers, and `check.sh` prints each with its sort and its two constants (`nsx.py equations`).

The translator follows each rounding operation with `canonStmt` or `canonStmt32`, which replaces a NaN result by Talos's canonical NaN through `lx_pun`.  N has no rule for the pair: its general rules give the comparison `t != t`, the store of the canonical NaN's word to the cell of `lx_pun`, and the read of the cell at `double` or `float`, forms that the translator also emits elsewhere.  The equations of the rounding operations therefore state the NaN that each operation returns, which CompCert computes by `binop_nan` with the choice function `Archi.choose_nan_64` or `choose_nan_32` of `aarch64/Archi.v` and the copy by its transcription of both.

Tests compare the two definitions of every equation.  `tests/native/clight.sh SEED COUNT aarch64` runs random programs of `tests/TestSupport/Native/ClightGen.lean` with `LClight.run` under `Archi.aarch64` and with `ccomp -interp` of a CompCert tree configured for AArch64, whose arithmetic is the extraction of `Floats.v`, and requires the same output.  The generator applies every operation of the table: the four arithmetic operators and the six comparisons at both widths, negation at both widths and `fabs` at `double`, `__builtin_fsqrt`, and the casts from `double` and `float` to `int` and `unsigned long long` and back from every integer type and between the two widths, with constants biased toward zeros, ±1, the largest finite number, subnormals, and the boundaries of conversions to integers, and a global `pun` that starts as a NaN or an infinity, with a chosen sign and payload, half of the time.  The fixed programs of `ClightGen.nanPrograms` cover the NaN rules: the default NaNs of both widths, a quiet and a signaling operand in both orders, the square root of a negative number, conversions of NaNs between the widths, negation and `fabs` of NaNs, and the binary32 square root through `double`.  A conversion out of range is undefined in CompCert, and the test requires the copy to be stuck there as well, so the partial conversions are compared where they are defined and where they are not.  `tests/native/clight-rocq.sh` evaluates the same programs with CompCert's own definitions in Rocq by `vm_compute` and compares them with the copy's output.  For binary32 numbers that are not NaNs, the copy's operations return Talos's results, and the exhaustive test of the A64 back end, `tests/baremetal/a64-fpexhaust.sh`, compares Talos's binary32 functions with the processor on every word, which supports the binary32 equations for numbers under the assumption, on which every program that CompCert compiles rests, that the processor computes what `Floats.v` computes.

## Rendering and the check

`uv run clightn/nsx.py render clightn/N.nsx clightn/rocq/NSpec.v LeanExe/Native/ClightN/Spec.lean` writes the two renderings of N with every argument of every constant explicit, and the check trusts none of its output.  For Rocq, `check.sh` generates `NRead.v`, which prints under `Set Printing All` each relation with its list of constructors, the type of the relation and of each constructor, the value and type of each constant, the value of each statement, the value and type of each equation's definition `eqn_NAME`, the type and the assumptions of each proof, and the assumptions of CompCert's `transf_c_program_correct`, each to a file of its own.  The reader of a group of planted errors in renderings prints no proofs and does not load `Compiler`.  For Lean, `ClightN.Read.dump` walks the elaborated expressions of the namespace `ClightN.Spec` and of the proofs in `ClightN.Proof` and writes them as S-expressions with every argument and the full name of every constant, together with the axioms of each proof.

`nsx.py check` maps each printed constant through the symbol table, turns numerals and strings into tokens, drops the type arguments of equality, `Some`, `None`, the existential, `cons`, `nil`, and `pair`, writes a product whose variable is unused as an implication, and replaces bound names by their depths.  It then compares every equation, relation, constructor, constant, and statement with the specification after the expansion of macros.  A constant outside the table never equals a term of the specification, so a coercion, an instance, or a definition that elaboration inserts appears as a difference.  The check also requires each relation to be inductive with exactly the constructors of the specification, each statement `S` to be proved by a theorem `NProof.S` in Rocq and `ClightN.Proof.S` in Lean whose type is the rendered statement itself, each carried statement to be the first premise of its Rocq theorem, and each proof to assume only what CompCert's `transf_c_program_correct` assumes in Rocq and `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` in Lean.  The Rocq theorem of a carried statement may also assume `ClassicalEpsilon.constructive_indefinite_description`, which CompCert's argument about behaviors uses.  `check.sh` runs the comparison once for each instance, against the printout of the Lean file that proves it.

`clightn/check.sh --mutations` then plants errors in copies of the renderings and the proofs and requires the check to report exactly those errors.  In the Lean rendering of N they are an operator, a numeral, a numeral of another type, the order of arguments, a coercion through `Nat`, a data constructor, the volatile flag of the output's variable, the order of a sequence's traces, the calling convention of `main`, the width of a word read from an array, a bound on a byte's offset, the size of a volatile element, the count of the loads that a trace reads, the statements before a loop, the index of a loop's invariant, a floating-point operation, a comparison, the initial bits of a `double` global, the bits that a store to a `float` cell writes, and the architecture of an equation's Lean constant.  In the Rocq rendering of N they are an operator, a coinductive relation, an extra constructor, the truth value of a branch, the association of a loop's traces, a membership without its premise, the size of a volatile element, the initial bytes of an array, the offset of a volatile load's event, the count of the loads before a store, the index of a loop's invariant, a conversion, a unary operator, the name of the built-in square root, and the operation of an equation's Rocq constant.  The instances' renderings receive a changed byte of the GCD trace and a changed argument of its call in Lean, a changed `main` and a changed argument in Rocq, a changed size of `lx_mem` in both provers for the SHA-256 program with input, and a changed count of its reads in Lean and a changed `main` in Rocq, and, for the controller program, a changed initial value of `lx_g0` and the swapped arguments of `app` in the equation of `P` in Lean, and a changed `main` and a changed argument of `P` in its first equation in Rocq.  `nsx.py` plants the errors of each other instance from the form of its statement: in each prover's rendering a changed identifier of `main` and a changed statement, the result 0 changed to 2 for a bare-metal program, and for a `main` that runs forever the swapped arguments of `app` in Lean and the changed argument of `P` in Rocq.  In each prover a theorem that proves a weaker statement under a statement's name and a proof that assumes its statement complete the set: `sorry` for two of N's statements, one of them on floating-point numbers, and in place of the proof of each instance's statement in Lean, and `Admitted` for two of N's statements, one of them on floating-point numbers, and in place of the `Qed` of each instance's theorem in Rocq.  A planted error that the comparison misses, or a difference that no planted error explains, fails the script.

## Running

The check needs `coqc`, a CompCert 3.18 tree configured for `aarch64-linux` with its proofs and `clightgen` built, `uv`, and the Lean toolchain, and it refuses a tree configured for another target.  It works in `build/clightn`, runs Lean through `tools/leanrun` with a memory limit of 38 GB, compiles the renderings of the instances outside the package with `lean -o`, and prints the agreement of the relations, constants, statements, and equations for each instance and then the list of the equations, followed with `--mutations` by a line for N's planted errors and one for each instance's.  With Lean at four threads, the rendering and proof of an instance take from 30 s for the controller to 127 s for the bare-metal SHA-256 program, 342 s for the gateway, whose proof peaks at 18 GB, of which the imports take about 5 GB, and 1,223 s for the Euler solver, whose proof peaks at 33 GB while the kernel checks the renaming of `inst_ok`.  Rocq compiles every file in about four minutes, and the planted errors take about a minute for each instance and nine minutes for the Euler solver, whose proof with `sorry` still runs `euler_steps`.  The whole check with `--mutations` takes about 62 minutes.  From the repository's root, the command is:

```sh
clightn/check.sh --mutations /path/to/CompCert
```

The proofs need `Archi.ptr64 = true`: on AArch64 a cast between two `long` types passes a pointer unchanged, as the constructors of `cast_ok` say, so the Rocq proofs fail against the ARMv7-M tree.  The x86-64 path has no CompCert tree with built proofs, so AArch64 is the only target that the check covers.  [`tools/full-check`](../tools/full-check) runs `clightn/check.sh --mutations` in step 7, after `rocq/check.sh`, when the CompCert tree with built proofs is configured for AArch64.
