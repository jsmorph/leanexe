# Gating and track association requirements: decisions and review

[The requirements](requirements.md) specify the first iteration of the gating demo from brief 6.3 of the space-operations candidates and from the plan that orders them, whose correction for 6.3 asks for the chi-square distribution through Mathlib's Gamma distribution, enclosures of its distribution function and quantile, an assignment theorem for the integer costs that a certificate check reads, and no maneuver detection until a theorem is defined.  This record gives each decision with its reason, the findings of a fresh agent's review of the requirements, and the findings of a second fresh agent's review of the statements of the Gaussian link, with the verification and the response for each.  Each decision followed the standard procedure: the options, a recommendation, a review of the recommendation, and a revision.

## Decisions

| Question | Options | Choice | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Definition of the chi-square distribution | A density of its own; Mathlib's Gamma distribution | `gammaMeasure (k/2) (1/2)` | The plan asks for it, and Mathlib's `cdf_gammaMeasure_eq_integral` gives the integral of the density, from which the fundamental theorem of calculus gives the closed form. |
| Closed form | Separate formulas for even and odd `k`; one recurrence in the shape | `cdf (k + 2) x = cdf k x - T(k/2, x/2)`, with the bases `k = 1` and `k = 2` | One proof for every shape `a > 0` gives both families, and the program's loop follows the same recurrence. |
| Quantity computed | The distribution function; the tail | The tail, with the distribution function as `1 - tail` | The terms of the tail are positive, so the tail keeps its relative accuracy where it is small, which is where a gate's level lies. |
| Odd degrees of freedom | `1 - erf(√y)` with the library's `erf`, as the conjunction demo uses; `erfc(√y)` with the library's `erfc` | `erfc` | With `erf` the tail's upper end never falls below `2.2e-16` for odd `k` (`[0, 2.2e-16]` at `k = 1`, `x = 100`, against `1.5e-23`), and the quantile gives status 2 for odd `k` at `p ≥ 1 - 2⁻⁵²`.  With `erfc` every tail from 0 to 1,450 lies within `8 (k + x + 10) 2⁻⁵³` relative, and every `p` up to `1 - 2⁻⁵³` gives status 0.  The cost is the seven tables of `erfc` in the module and a longer compilation. |
| Points beyond the normal range of `exp(-x/2)` | The closed form at every `x`; the closed form up to a cutoff and the tail's monotonicity beyond it | Cutoff at 1,450 | Beyond 1,490 `exp(-x/2)` rounds to 0, and the closed form's upper end reaches 1 (`[0, 1]` for the tail at `x = 1e300`).  The tail decreases, so `[0, upper end at 1,450]` contains it. |
| Overflow of the terms | Reject; clamp each term to `[0, 1]` | Clamp | `Model.term_le_one` proves the real terms at most 1, and the clamp keeps every finite `x` from overflowing. |
| Quantile's level | The distribution function at `p`; the tail at `α` | `p` | The standard quantile.  A level `α` enters as `1 - α`, which [the requirements](requirements.md#the-quantile) state as a limit. |
| Bracket of the quantile | Growth by a factor, as in the radius program; one fixed upper end | `B = k + 2√(40k) + 80` | Laurent and Massart's inequality puts the tail at `B` below `exp(-40)`.  The search needs no growth loop, and no theorem depends on `B`. |
| Midpoint of a halving | Arithmetic; of the bit patterns | Bit patterns | 63 halvings reach adjacent doubles from any bracket of nonnegative doubles, so tiny quantiles need no more halvings than large ones. |
| Reuse of the conjunction demo | Copy its interval enclosure of `exp` and its literal lemmas; import them; move them to the interval library | Import | Moving them changes another demo's files, and copying duplicates proofs.  The move is an open item. |
| Gate's covariance | Packed lower triangle; full matrix with a symmetry check | Full `d × d` with `s[i·d + j] ≤ s[j·d + i]` and the reverse | The real matrix is then the array's, which `Matrix.PosDef` requires to be symmetric. |
| Positive definiteness | Interval Cholesky with square roots; pivots of symmetric Gaussian elimination | Pivots | It generalizes the conjunction demo's conditional variance, needs no square root, and `Model.posDef_of_pivots` proves positive definiteness from the LDLᵀ form of the quadratic form. |
| Storage of the elimination | In place with nested loops; new arrays by `LeanExe.build` at each step | `build` | `build_get` states each entry, so the proof needs no invariant of an inner loop.  The cost is an allocation of `d² + d` intervals per step. |
| The probability outside the gate | Computed by each call of `gate`; computed once by `tail d γ` | `tail d γ` of the module `chisquare` | The probability depends only on `d` and `γ`, which a tracker fixes once for a gate, while the verdict runs for each pair of a track and an observation.  The gate's module then needs neither `exp` nor `erfc`, and it compiles in minutes instead of half an hour. |
| Dimension | Up to 6; up to 64 | Up to 64, with the measured acceptance region stated | The theorem holds for every accepted result, and the table of the requirements tells a caller which covariances the elimination resolves. |
| Assignment's form | Rectangular with one-sided duals; square with forbidden pairs | Square, with the mark `2⁶³ - 1` | Rectangular problems with dummy rows and columns reduce to it, and the theorem concerns the admissible permutations directly. |
| Integer range | Check each sum for overflow; bound the duals | `|u|, |v| < 2⁶²` | Every sum `u[i] + v[j]` is then exact in `Int64` and below the mark, and the program computes no total. |
| Route of the Gaussian link | The density of the standard Gaussian, polar coordinates, and the volume of the ball; induction on `d` with the convolution of Gamma densities; characteristic functions; moment-generating functions below `1/2` with the identity theorem | Moment-generating functions | Mathlib at the pinned revision has no density of the standard Gaussian on a Euclidean space, for which the first route was estimated at 600 to 900 lines.  The convolution route needs the density of a convolution and the Beta integral.  The characteristic function of the Gamma law needs a Gamma integral with a complex rate, and Mathlib's has a real rate.  The integrals of `exp(t x)` for `t < 1/2` are all in Mathlib, and [the Gaussian model](Gaussian.lean) takes 219 lines. |
| Uniqueness from moment-generating functions | Mathlib's `eqOn_complexMGF_of_mgf`; a lemma on a half-plane | `Model.ext_of_mgf_Iio` | Mathlib's lemma needs the moment-generating functions equal on all of `ℝ`, which for `t ≥ 1/2` would need proofs that both integrands are not integrable.  The lemma needs the values for `t < c` only. |
| Whitening factor | `CFC.sqrt S`; the factor of the elimination in [the Mahalanobis model](Mahalanobis.lean) | `CFC.sqrt S` | `multivariateGaussian` is defined through `CFC.sqrt S`, so the image is direct, and another factor would need a proof that its image is the same law. |
| Form of the Gaussian theorem | The tail for `γ ≥ 0`; the equality of laws and the tail for every `γ` | `Model.gaussian_chiSquare` and `Model.gaussian_tail` for every `γ` | The equality of laws gives every event, and `γ ≥ 0` is unnecessary.  `d ≥ 1` stays, since `Model.chiSquare 0` is the zero measure. |
| The gate's probability | A conclusion of `gate_sound`; separate theorems; separate theorems and a conclusion of `gate_module` | `gate_miss` and `gate_miss_tail` in [the theorems of a missed association](Miss.lean), and a conclusion of `gate_module` | `gate_sound` remains a statement about the program, and `gate_module`, which states the module's results, states the probability.  `gate_miss_tail` joins the gate with the tail of the module `chisquare`. |

## Review

A fresh agent reviewed the draft against the brief, the plan, the VKB entry on independent review, and the repository, and it ran no Lean.  It built a model of the interval library that follows `Library/Numerics/Bounds/Program.lean` and `Library/Numerics/Interval/Program.lean` bit for bit, with an adversarial mode that moves each library value one step toward the interior before widening, mpmath oracles at 40 digits, a model of the gate, a model of the check with its own Hungarian method and a successive-shortest-path oracle, and wrong programs for each part.  It found the chi-square model, the recurrence, `T(1/2, y)`, the term bound, the bracket, the bisection, and the count of multiplications correct, and no containment failure in its models.  Its scripts are in the agent's scratch directory, and the table gives each finding with its verification and response.

| Finding | Verification | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 1. `tail` and `cdf` reject for odd `k` at `x` from 0 to `2.5e-323`, because `mul` moves the lower end of a product that rounds to 0 one step below 0 and `sqrt` rejects it, and `gate` returns status 2 for `g = 0` at odd `d`. | 3,496 of 49,000 lanes of its model reject; with `y` and `y/π` clamped to `[0, x]`, none does. | Adopted.  The program clamps both, the interface states that every finite `x` with `k` in range gives status 0, and the test requires it.  The program had the defect, which the distribution test also found at `x = 0`. |
| 2. The quantile gives status 2 for every odd `k` at `p ≥ 1 - 2⁻⁵²`, because `erf` rounds to 1 and its widened lower end is `1 - 2⁻⁵²`. | 1,000 of 24,000 lanes; with the clamp and `erfc`, none. | Adopted: `erfc` for odd `k`, which the decisions above record. |
| 3. The stated accuracy does not match the method: odd tails lose relative accuracy at every level below `1e-6`, even tails become `[0, 1]` where `exp(-y)` rounds to 0, the width of `cdf` reaches `5.3e-13` at `k = 1000`, and the quantile's width grows near `p = 1`. | Its model on 536 lanes and on points far in the tail. | Adopted.  With `erfc` and the cutoff at 1,450, the requirements state the measured bounds `8 (k + x + 10) u`, `4 (k + 10) u`, and `2 (k + 10) u / min(p, 1 - p)`, the region beyond 1,450, the behavior below `p ≈ k u`, and that a level below `2⁻⁵³` cannot be expressed. | 4. | 5. The gate accepts a region of covariances that shrinks with `d`: every matrix up to a condition number of `1e12` for `d ≤ 9`, none above `1e4` at `d = 64`. | A sweep of random positive definite matrices, with no containment failure and no accepted matrix that is not positive definite. | Adopted as a stated limit, with the sweep's table in the requirements. | 6. A Cholesky factorization in mpmath at 40 digits accepts an exactly singular matrix, so the reference misjudges the singular cases. | A rank-5 integer matrix of order 6. | Adopted.  The reference decides positive definiteness from the pivots of the elimination in exact rational arithmetic. |

| 8. The gate test omits most causes of status 1, an overflow, and an asymmetry between entries that are not adjacent, which a check of adjacent entries misses. | Its wrong programs. | Adopted: the cases hold each cause.  A rejected tail no longer occurs, since `tail` accepts every finite `γ` with `d` in range. |
| 9. Several mutations of the assignment test do not reach the status of the table: a repeated column usually fails tightness first, and the mutations must use `perm[i] = n` and `±2⁶²` exactly to detect `>` in place of `≥`. | Its model of the check on its instances. | Adopted. | 10. | 13. The proof needs no density, as the decision on its route states. |
| 15. The model and the remaining numbers check out; "one exp" is two calls, the first even term is a tail rather than a difference of tails, and the test names no `h`. | mpmath at 40 digits on 966 pairs, Crossref for the citation. | Adopted: the wording is corrected. |

### Wrong programs

The review's table of wrong programs, with the cases of the revised tests:

| Wrong program | Cases that detect it |
|---|---|
| Tail: `y = x`; no factor 2 in the odd first term; parity exchanged; one term too many or too few; odd divisor `2j + 2`; divisor `2j` | Containment of the reference at the test's points |
| Tail that always returns `[0, 1]`, or always rejects | The requirement of status 0 and the width bound |
| Quantile: lower end moved when the enclosure's lower end lies below `p`; upper end moved when `p` lies at or below its upper end; result's upper end from the first bisection | Containment |
| Quantile: arithmetic midpoint; a single bisection | The width bound |
| Quantile: a bracket with `t = 10` | Status 0 near `p = 1` |
| Gate: no symmetry check; a symmetry check of adjacent entries; no pivot test; the original diagonal as pivot; `r` not updated; the tail at `d - 1` | The asymmetric cases, the indefinite and random cases, and containment |
| Gate: verdict 1 on `hi < γ`; verdict 2 on `γ ≤ lo` | The thresholds at the ends of an earlier interval, and `ν = 0` with `γ = 0` |
| Gate: no check of `g`'s finiteness or of `g < 0` | The cases `g = NaN` and `g = -1`, by status |
| Check: no repetition test; a repetition test between adjacent rows; a repetition reported at the earlier row | The tight repetition, the repetition in rows 0 and 2, and the row of the model |
| Check: feasibility only on the assignment's pairs | The lowered cost of one pair |
| Check: tightness as `u + v ≤ c`; a tie rejected | The exchanged columns and the solver's certificates |
| Check: equality of the total cost, or no range check, with wrapping sums | The values out of range and the ends of the range |
| Check: a closed range `[-2⁶², 2⁶²]`; a column test `perm[i] > n` | `u[n - 1] = 2⁶²` and `perm[0] = n` |
| Check: no mark test in pass 2 | Status 5 in place of 3 for the forbidden pair of row 0 |

No case detects these classes:

- A check without the mark test in pass 3 is equivalent to the program, which has no such test.
- A check whose limit on `n` is 65,535 behaves the same, since no array of `c` reaches `2³²` words.
- A gate that tests `0 < p.hi` in place of `0 < p.lo` is equivalent, since `div` rejects a divisor that holds 0, and so is one that reads row `k` in place of column `k`, whose real values are equal.
- A gate that uses `mul` in place of `sq`, and different groupings of the term update, change only bits and widths, which the module test pins to the Lean source.
- A quantile that moves its lower end when an enclosure rejects, or that omits the evaluation at `B`, gives the same results, since no evaluation rejects and the tail at `B` lies below every representable `1 - p`.  The proof excludes the first.

## Review of the Gaussian link

A fresh agent reviewed the statements of `Model.gaussian_chiSquare`, `Model.gaussian_tail`, `gate_miss`, `gate_miss_tail`, and `gate_module` against the requirements and Mathlib's definitions, and it checked its claims with a Lean file of examples on the built modules.  It found the statements correct, their hypotheses necessary, and none of them vacuous.

| Finding | Verification | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 1. `multivariateGaussian 0 S` is the normal law with mean 0 and covariance `S` for a positive definite `S`, and the scoped instances of `CFC.sqrt` cannot change the measure in the statements. | Lean: `integral_id_multivariateGaussian`, `covariance_eval_multivariateGaussian`, and the `IsGaussian` instance for the measure; `gaussian_tail` restated and proved in a file that opens neither scoped instance.  The constant's only instance arguments are `Fintype` and `DecidableEq`. | None needed. |
| 2. The event `γ < Model.mahalanobis S ν` is the complement of the gate, but the probability concerns the real gate, while the verdict 0 occurs on both sides of it, so a tracker that treats the verdict 0 as a rejection has a miss rate that no theorem bounds. | The verdict conclusions of `gate_sound`. | Adopted: [the README's limits](README.md#limits-of-the-theorems) state it. |
| 3. Each hypothesis is needed: for `d = 0` the identity fails at `γ = -1`, since `Model.chiSquare 0 = 0`, and for `d ≥ 1` and a matrix that is not positive definite it fails at `γ = 0`.  No hypothesis on `γ` is needed. | Lean: `Model.chiSquare 0 = 0`, every matrix of order 0 positive definite, `Model.tail 0 (-1) = 0`, and the case `S = 0` at `d = 1`. | None needed; the requirements state no hypothesis on `γ`. |
| 4. In `gate_miss` and `gate_module`, the matrix is the one whose positive definiteness `gate_sound` proves, `γ` is the value that the verdict uses, and `d` is the size of `nu`. | The statements and the proof of `gate_miss`. | None needed. |
| 5. `k.toNat = nu.size` links the calls of `gate_miss_tail` exactly, and `Contains` claims nothing beyond `tail_sound`.  The hypothesis that `tail k g` accepts is needed, since no theorem states that `tail` accepts every finite point. | `tail_sound` and `Contains`. | None needed. |
| 6. No statement is vacuous: the measure is a probability measure, the event is measurable, and `gate [3, 4] [1, 0, 0, 1] 25` accepts. | Lean: `probReal_univ` for the measure; the module test for the call. | None needed. |
| 7. `gate_module` kept five of the twelve conclusions of `gate_sound`, dropping `s.size = d·d` and the finiteness of the endpoints, while its docstring and the README stated all of them. | The statement of `gate_module`. | Adopted: `gate_module` states every conclusion of `gate_sound` and then the probability. |
| 8. The README, the requirements, and this record described the link as unproved. | The documents as the review read them. | Adopted: the documents state the proved theorems and the route of the proof. |

## Open items

1. The quantile of the tail at a level `α`, for levels near or below `2⁻⁵³`, and a direct series for the distribution function at small `p`.
2. A tighter enclosure of the Mahalanobis distance for larger `d`.
3. A move of the conjunction demo's `expI`, its soundness, and the literal lemmas to the interval library, where both demos would import them.
4. Maneuver detection, once its theorem about false alarms is defined.
