# Passive-safety check requirements: decisions and review

[The requirements](requirements.md) specify the passive-safety check from brief 4.1 of the space-operations candidates and from section 2 of the plan that orders them.  This record gives each decision with its reason and the findings of two reviews by fresh agents, one of the requirements and one of the panel enclosure and the accuracy, with the verification and the response for each finding.  Each decision followed the standard procedure: the options, a recommendation, a review of the recommendation, and a revision.

## Decisions

| Question | Options | Choice | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Input form | Cartesian state in the chief's local-vertical, local-horizontal frame; relative orbital elements | Cartesian | The closed form is the model's definition, and a Cartesian state enters it without a further map.  Navigation filters give Cartesian states, and for a circular chief relative orbital elements are a linear change of the closed form's constants, which a converter outside the theorems can supply. |
| Navigation error | A point state; a box of states; an ellipsoid of a covariance | A box of initial states, and a box of velocity changes for each burn | Interval arithmetic encloses a box directly, and the burn boxes cover execution error with no change of structure.  A point is a box of zero width. |
| Horizon and thresholds | A horizon for each scenario from its failure; one horizon from the epoch | One horizon `[0, T]` for every scenario, a sphere of radius `dmin`, and a second threshold `drc` for the radial and cross-track distance after each scenario's last burn, for all time | One horizon keeps the statement short, and the caller chooses `T` after the last burn plus the drift to check.  `check_passive` joins the two bounds into a bound of the separation for all time. |
| Missed burn | Omission, with the later burns executed; free drift after the failed burn; both | Both: scenarios `1` to `N` omit one burn, and scenarios `N + 1` to `2N` stop before one burn | The plan's words, "each plan with one burn missed", read as omission, and passive safety in Fehse's account is the free drift after a failure.  Both readings cost twice the searches and leave no ambiguity. |
| Bound for all time | The closed form of the minimum for bounded motion; subdivision over the phase | Subdivision over the phase of one period after the last burn | The radial and cross-track components of every closed-form trajectory are periodic, drifting or not, so the bound holds for all time without the condition `vy = −2n x`, which a box of states does not meet. |
| Search | Uniform panels; an adaptive march that halves and doubles its step; branch and bound with a stack | The adaptive march, with `fuel` panels for each search and the smallest step `e·2⁻⁴⁰` | The march allocates nothing and keeps one record of state, and its status separates a bound that was not shown from a search that ran out of fuel. |
| Enclosure of a panel | The natural interval form; a mean-value form of the squared distance in time; a mean-value form of each component; a second-order Taylor form | The Taylor form of the squared distance with the Lagrange remainder, intersected with the Taylor form of `x² + z²` plus the component bound of `y²` and with the component bounds, and the natural form on a separation panel that contains a burn time | A model of the program, with the library's outward rounding, measured each form on the accepted cases with the accuracy below.  At an accuracy of 0.1 m the natural form needs 102,007 panels for the safety ellipse, the mean-value forms of `x² + y² + z²` and of `x² + z²` with the same intersections 3,462, and the Taylor form 1,526, and the other cases show the same order.  The natural form loses the cancellation of the closed form's terms over an interval of time, and the full Taylor form alone loses the ellipse's along-track spread, which the bound of `x² + z²` removes.  Between burns the trajectory is smooth, so the Taylor form needs only `Model.flow_hasDerivAt`, and the natural form on panels that contain a burn time costs at most a few panels. |
| Refinement past the threshold | Stop at the threshold; refine to a fixed relative accuracy; refine to an accuracy that the caller gives | Refine to the caller's accuracy `acc`, a length: a panel above the smallest step is accepted only when `√ℓ ≥ √M − acc` | Stopping at the threshold leaves the bounds near the thresholds whatever the enclosure, 50.06 m for the ellipse against a minimum of 92.59 m.  A length states the accuracy in the units of the thresholds, and a large `acc` restores the threshold-only search. |
| Reference of the accuracy | The panel's own midpoint; the least midpoint bound of the search; the least midpoint bound of every search of the same kind | The least midpoint bound `M` of the searches of the same kind, carried from scenario to scenario | A panel's own midpoint makes every panel resolve the function's variation, even far above the minimum.  The bound that the program returns is the least over the scenarios, and on the accepted cases with burns the shared record needs 23% to 50% fewer panels than a record for each search at accuracies of 1 m and 0.1 m, as the second review measured. |
| Accuracy 0 | Require `acc > 0`; allow `acc = 0` and state its cost | Allow `acc ≥ 0` | An `acc` far below the excess of the panels' intervals exhausts the fuel as `acc = 0` does, so excluding 0 alone would not remove the cost, and status 5 reports it.  The requirements state that `acc = 0` covers every stretch of decreasing midpoint bounds at the smallest step. |
| Form of `acc` | A third argument; a value of the array | A third argument, beside `fuel` | `acc` controls the search, as `fuel` does, and the array's layout, `InBox`, and `Plan` stay as they are.  The theorems' statements gain the argument and keep their conclusions. |
| Target | Ground (WebAssembly, C); an AArch64 image; a Cortex-M7 image | Ground | The Cortex-M7 generator rejects floating point.  The program allocates nothing and bounds its cost, so an AArch64 image in the form of the tabulators' images is a later increment. |

## Review

A fresh agent reviewed the draft against the brief, the plan, and the repository.  It built its own model of `check` from the requirements, with an interval arithmetic that follows the library's, an oracle that integrates the equations with a fourth-order Runge–Kutta method, and mpmath checks at 40 digits, and it ran no Lean.  It found the closed form correct sign by sign, the superposition formula, the periodicity of the radial and cross-track components, the scenario table, the cost bound, and the facts about the repository correct, and it found no soundness violation of its model on 200 random plans.  Its scripts are in the agent's scratch directory, and the table gives each finding with its verification and response.

| Finding | Verification | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The phrase "the derivatives that the equations require" does not define the derivatives of the velocity components.  Of 24 sign mutants of the closed form, all fail the derivative check but the negated `y` term of `y(τ)`, which only `flow_zero` excludes. | The derivative check at 40 digits and the mutants | The requirements state the first-order system, and `Model.Solves` states it for all six components.  Both `flow_hasDerivAt` and `flow_zero` are theorems. |
| 2. The sentence on D'Amico and Montenbruck's minimum claims an equality that holds only for a point state, and the text does not say why the brief's closed form for bounded motion is not used. | The minimum over the phase equals the smallest singular value of `[[x, vx/n], [z, vz/n]]` on 300 bounded states | Reworded: `rc` is a lower bound of that minimum over the box, and a box does not satisfy `vy = −2n x`. |
| 3. The claim that the lower end of a squared distance is never negative is false: outward rounding moves an exact 0 one step below 0, and the upper bound of `0²` is one step above 0, so a zero threshold rejects a V-bar hold. | The library's `endpoint` and the review's model | Confirmed, and the program had the defect.  `nonneg` clamps a squared distance to its part at or above 0, `square` gives the bound 0 for a zero threshold, and the requirements state both.  A zero threshold accepts every panel, and the case `vbarHold` is accepted. |
| 4. The radial and cross-track test applies to every scenario, including the initial orbit, so V-bar approaches need `drc = 0`.  The text does not state that the two bounds give a separation of at least `min(dmin, drc)` for all time. | Scenario `N + 1` is the initial orbit; every burn lies in `[0, T]` | Both are stated.  The test stays for every scenario, with `drc = 0` for plans that cross the along-track axis, and `check_passive` proves the bound for all time. |
| 5. The box of the state at the last burn loses the correlation of position and velocity and makes the radial and cross-track bound conservative: it rejects at `drc = 0` some plans whose sampled minimum is 24 m, and on 200 random plans it accepts 90 where a direct evaluation accepts 170. | The review's model of both methods against the oracle | Adopted.  The search evaluates the position formula at `t_L + φ/n` from the initial box and the burn boxes, and each term is periodic in `φ`.  The program no longer encloses the full state, `Model.flow_phase` replaces the phase lemma of the state, and the cost bound becomes `2(2N + 1)(N + 1) fuel`. |
| 6. The text leaves open the order of a burn's six velocity values, the result types, the meaning of `stop` for statuses 4 and 6, the operations that bound `dmin²` and round the square root, and the grouping of the interval expression. | Reading of the text | Each is stated: values in pairs for each component, the result types, `stop` as a phase, the outward-rounded product and the lower end of the interval square root, and the grouping of `flowPosition` in `Closed.lean`. |
| 7. The status of the last unit of fuel, of a threshold whose square overflows, and of a rejection outside a panel is undefined, and so is the order of the two searches. | The review's model at fuel 722 and 723 and at `dmin = 1e200` | The fuel is tested before each panel, so a panel that fails at the smallest step on the last unit gives status 3.  An overflowing square gives status 7 at scenario 0 with `stop` 0.  The redesign of finding 5 leaves no rejection outside a panel, and the separation search runs first. |
| 8. `sep` can lie one step below `dmin`, and a program that returns `sep = rc = 0` satisfies every theorem. | `outSqrt` of `1 + 2⁻⁵²` is below 1 | `sep` is the larger of `dmin` and the rounded-down square root, and `check_sound` states `dmin ≤ sep` and `drc ≤ rc`, which excludes the zero result. |
| 9. `check_sound` needs no further premise for soundness, but its conclusion does not state the input's size, the order of the burns, or their range, and a program that always rejects satisfies the theorems. | Reading of the theorem against the program | `check_input` states the size, that the box and the plan's boxes are not empty, and that every matching list of burns is ordered in `[0, T]`.  Characterizations of statuses 1 and 2 would restate the program's tests, so the cases require the statuses and scenarios instead, and the requirements state which programs the theorems admit. |
| 10. No test produces status 6, the rejection tests do not require the scenario, and cases with burns at multiples of `π/n` and zero components let 11 sign mutants pass. | The review's wrong programs on its cases | The cases fix status and scenario, include the review's cases with generic phases and nonzero components, and include the review's input for status 6. |
| 11. The cost bound is correct for the method as written. | Count of closed forms per scenario | The bound is restated for the method of finding 5. |
| 12. The references and the facts about the repository are correct, the conjunction demo is cited but not used, Fehse is not cited in the text, and seven paragraphs have fewer than three sentences. | Crossref, `docs/m7-bare-metal.md`, `docs/a64-bare-metal.md`, `Library/Numerics/Interval/Functions.lean` | The text cites Fehse for the drift scenarios, drops the conjunction demo, and joins the short paragraphs. |

### Wrong programs

The review ran each wrong program in its model on its proposed cases, which the cases of the module test include under the names below.  The program gives the expected status and scenario on every case, and in every rejection with status 3 or 4 the integration test finds a sample of the boxes that violates the threshold, so a wrong program that accepts one of these cases contradicts `check_sound`.

| Wrong program | Cases that detect it |
|---|---|
| Only the nominal scenario | `missedDrift`, `freeDrift`, `laterBurn`, `genericK2`, `genericL` accept |
| No drift scenarios | `freeDrift` accepts |

| `t − t_k` without the maximum with 0 | `lateBurn` and `genericM`, through the scenario number |

| Omission scenarios that also drop the later burns | `laterBurn`, `genericL` accept; `freeDrift` reports scenario 1 |
| The centres of the boxes only | `boxRc`, `genericK1`, `genericM` accept |

No case detects these classes:

- A program that computes `t − t_k` without the maximum, for `N ≤ 2`, never accepts an unsafe plan, since every segment of its trajectories between burns is a segment of a drift scenario, which it computes correctly.  The scenario numbers of `lateBurn` and `genericM` detect it.
- A program that skips scenario `2N`, or scenario `N + 1` when `N = 1`, gives the same results, since those scenarios duplicate scenario `N` and scenario `1`.
- A radial and cross-track search that ends at the double nearest `2π` instead of the one above it misses phases 2.4e-16 rad wide, where the distance is continuous and equal at `0` and `2π`.  Only the proof excludes it.
- A program more conservative than the method, with another grouping, a coarser smallest step, or another step policy, differs only in borderline statuses, `stop`, and the bounds.  The module test compares the module with native Lean bit for bit, and the proof fixes the program.

## Review of the panel enclosure and the accuracy

A fresh agent reviewed the requirements of the Taylor form and the accuracy before the program depended on them, with the request, the requirements, `Closed.lean`, the interval library, and the cases, and without the author's reasoning or models.  It built its own model of `check xs fuel acc` with the library's outward rounding and an oracle that propagates the system by its matrix exponential, which agrees with a Runge–Kutta integration to 8.7e-12 relative.  Its oracle gives three minima for each case: the sampled minimum of the integration test, the least distance over the whole product of boxes, and the componentwise bound that the record `M` approaches.  Its model reproduces 50.17 m for the natural form, the decisions' 50.06 m, 102,007 panels, and 1,526 panels, and finds no bound above the oracle's minimum on the cases, on 600 random plans at three accuracies, or on 443 plans with thresholds within 3% of the minima.  It ran no Lean.  The case list changed during the review, and the agent ran its model again on the revised list.

|---|---|---|
| 1. `fuelRc` returns status 5 instead of 6 at a finite accuracy: with fuel 1 its separation search must accept `[0, 100]` in one panel, which the accuracy refines. | The model at accuracies `1e300`, 1, and 0.1 and horizons of 100, 1, and 0.1 s | Each case has its own accuracy, and `fuelRc` runs at `1e300`.  Every case of the list keeps its expected status and scenario. |
