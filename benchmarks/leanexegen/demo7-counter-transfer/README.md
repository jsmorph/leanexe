# Demo 7 counter-transfer benchmark

Demo 7 is a held-out scalar-loop case generated from a prose request on 2026-08-08.  Its public function is the identity on `Array UInt64`, but a singleton passes through a separate scalar helper that transfers its value by decrementing one counter and incrementing another.  This gives the artifact proof a two-accumulator invariant, wrapping addition and subtraction, and a decreasing measure without adding application-specific mathematics.

The reference package binds a 1,750-byte WASM module with SHA-256 digest `f437ebc16e352391ff05ff79d957eb7ef5652424d6d28c3f279e782980eeb7a5`.  Existing scalar post-test annotations exposed the exact loop, compact transitions, scalar entry, and complete singleton wrapper, but the proof agent derived the fixed-width counter arithmetic itself.  Stage 5 took 577.039 seconds, produced a 171-line proof, and required four edited candidates.

The retained LTG adds `State.localU64ToNat`, `CounterTransition.decrement_add_increment`, and `CounterTransition.decrement_toNat_lt`.  The recipe reports the counter theorems only when the checked scalar descriptor contains both unit decrement and unit increment, and it reports `U64Op.apply` when a generated transition contains a descriptor binary operation.  These declarations depend on scalar values and operations rather than the generated function, frame size, or public specification.

Three retained runs completed in 520.815, 405.284, and 816.771 seconds.  Their 520.815-second median is 56.224 seconds, or 9.7 percent, below the reference, while their 411.486-second range records substantial agent variance.  The proofs contain 125, 135, and 167 lines, giving a 135-line median, and the journals record three, one, and ten edited candidates.  The host UTC clock moved during several runs, so every comparison uses the monotonic durations stored in proof telemetry rather than differences between wall-clock timestamps.

The slow retained journal identified unstable composition between `postTestProgram_spec` and the generated scalar-entry theorem.  A generated `terminates_with_of_post_test` theorem moved that composition into checked annotation support and passed exact-artifact verification.  Three screens then completed in 387.160, 558.581, and 748.263 seconds, giving a 558.581-second median that is 7.3 percent above the retained configuration, so the active recipe omits the theorem and the `composed-*` packages preserve the rejected experiment.

A deterministic cut-point starter next derived a scalar result function from the formal singleton result, applied the complete wrapper theorem, and left the scalar proof followed by two formal-result equations.  Its only screen took 936.788 seconds, 79.9 percent above the retained median, and produced a 155-line proof after four edits.  The active starter therefore remains at the checked wrapper boundary, while `cutpoint-rejected-1` preserves the accepted proof and journal from this rejected structure.

That journal exposed a coordinate mismatch between combined-local indices in scalar annotations and indices into `State.locals`.  A candidate `combinedLocalU64ToNat` helper accepted the annotation coordinate directly and all three agents used it, reducing edited candidates to one, two, and one.  The runs took 651.892, 577.172, and 521.718 seconds, whose 577.172-second median is 10.8 percent above the retained median, so the helper and automatic recipe selection were removed and the `combined-measure-*` packages preserve the screen.

The next experiment selected detailed catalog entries and strategy sections only for functions left uncovered by a checked whole-function composition.  It reduced the Demo 7 prompt and supplied guidance from about 13,500 words to about 6,800 words, selecting `Array`, `ScalarTransition`, and `FixedArraySingletonWrapper` while retaining the other permitted modules as named fallbacks.  Both agents followed the selected composition and scalar recipe without a fallback, but their runs took 777.102 and 818.470 seconds, giving a two-run median of 797.786 seconds, 53.2 percent above the retained median.  A third run could not lower the three-run median below 777.102 seconds, so the active generator restored the full context and the `focused-context-*` packages preserve the rejected proofs and journals.

`scalar-current-1` preserves the initial reference.  `ltg-candidate-1` preserves the first counter-helper screen before `U64Op.apply` became an operation-selected recipe declaration, and `ltg-final-1` through `ltg-final-3` preserve the promoted configuration.  The `composed-*`, `cutpoint-rejected-1`, `combined-measure-*`, and `focused-context-*` packages preserve rejected changes.
