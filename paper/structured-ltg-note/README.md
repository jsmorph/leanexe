# Structured LTG research note

`main.tex` and `references.bib` produce the short research note *Structured Retrieval for a Growing Lean Artifact-Proof Library*.  The note reports the repository's structured LTG design, the two fixed-artifact Demo 6 trials, and the synthetic 10,000-record selectivity test.  `main.pdf` is the compiled submission file.

Build the paper by running `pdflatex main`, `bibtex main`, and two more `pdflatex main` passes in this directory.  The submitted title, author, and abstract must match the PDF text and metadata.  marXiv published the reviewed replacement with no remarks as [marXiv:2608.00029](http://localhost:8000/abs/2608.00029).
