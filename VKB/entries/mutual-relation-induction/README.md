# Induction over mutually inductive execution relations

Two semantics in the repository define execution as a pair of mutually inductive relations: `LeanExe.Wasi.Exec` and `LoopExec` for WASI actions, and the Clight copy's `exec_stmt` and `eval_funcall` for the native path.  A lemma about every execution needs induction over both relations at once.  Lean rejects the ordinary methods, and the WASI and bare-metal proofs use the forms below.

For `Exec` and `LoopExec`, Lean rejects a recursive proof over the two relations, and the `induction` tactic does not apply to them.  Apply the shared recursor with explicit motives, `refine Exec.rec (motive_1 := fun {α} action world trace outcome final _ => …) (motive_2 := fun {α σ} remaining index state test step next world trace outcome final _ => …) ?_ … execution`, with one goal for each constructor of both relations, as `LeanExe.Wasi.Exec.not_trapped` and `LeanExe.Wasi.Exec.bind` do.  Each motive binds every index of its relation, the implicit types included, and the second motive needs its own type variable `σ` because a loop's state type differs from the action's result.  Proofs over the resource semantics fail on motives left unopened in a case, on witness names reused across cases, and on existential predicates left uninstantiated, so each case must open its motive and name its variables afresh.  The resource semantics has three relations and follows the same form with `motive_3`, as `Verified.Wasi.Resource.ResourceExec.erase` shows.  A lemma about one loop can instead induct on the loop's remaining count and invert the derivation at each count, as `LeanExe.Wasi.LoopExec.resume` does.

In the Clight copy, Lean's structural recursion over `LClight.exec_stmt` and `eval_funcall` needs every index of the recursive argument to be a variable.  Write the two theorems in a `mutual` block that matches on the derivation and recurses on its sub-derivations, and replace a literal index by a variable with an equation: `LClight.exec_stmt_ren` in `LeanExe/Native/Proof/Rename.lean` takes `{e : env} (he : e = empty_env)` where its statement would otherwise say `empty_env`.  Put a second relation between the derivation's statement and another one after the derivation as its own hypothesis and split it with `match`, as `LClight.exec_export_core` in `LeanExe/Native/Proof/Export.lean` takes `CoreRel s' c`.  Running `cases` on the derivation's indices first rewrites the derivation's type, and Lean then rejects the recursive calls on it, so keep each derivation as it is and state the equations that `cases` would substitute as separate facts.  `LeanExe.Native.exec_quiet` and `funcall_quiet` in `LeanExe/Native/Proof/Quiet.lean` follow the same form, and so do `ClightN.Proof.exec_to_n` and `ClightN.Proof.call_to_n` in `LeanExe/Native/ClightN/Exec.lean`, which carry the copy's executions to those of N and take `e = empty_env` as a premise after the colon.

The copy's evaluation of expressions, `LClight.eval_expr` and `LClight.eval_lvalue`, is mutually inductive as well, and a proof about the expressions of a fixed set of forms can recurse on the expression instead of on the derivation.  `ClightN.Proof.eval_to_n` in `LeanExe/Native/ClightN/Exec.lean` takes the expression as its recursive argument, with `termination_by structural a => a`, and matches nested patterns for the shapes that the translator emits, such as `.Ederef (.Ebinop op base i pt) ty` for an element of an array.  Each arm inverts the derivation with `cases`, which the recursion on the expression leaves free, and recurses on the subexpressions.  The proof lists the arms of the other constructors one by one, nested ones included, and closes each by the Boolean check `exprOk`, which excludes them.  A lemma that inverts one part of a derivation takes the facts about its subexpressions as functions, as `ClightN.Proof.eval_elem_addr` takes them for the base and the index of an element's address, and `ClightN.Proof.baseOk_spec` turns the Boolean check of an array's base into the two shapes that it accepts.

The determinism proofs in `LeanExe.Native.Clight.Determinism` compare two derivations in the same form.  `LClight.exec_stmt_determ_prefix` and `LClight.eval_funcall_determ_prefix` form a `mutual` block, each ending with `termination_by structural h₁`, with the second derivation and the other hypotheses after the colon.  Each proof starts with `match h₁ with`, introduces the rest in each arm, and runs `cases` on the second derivation.  `cases h₁` in place of the `match` fails with "failed to infer structural recursion", because it rewrites the derivation that the recursion needs.  Lean also promotes the uniform leading indices of a non-recursive inductive to parameters, such as `chunk m b ofs` of `volatile_load`, so `cases … with | ctor names` takes fewer names than the constructor's binders show and extra names fail with "Too many variable names provided".  `LClight.volatile_load_determ` shows the right counts.
