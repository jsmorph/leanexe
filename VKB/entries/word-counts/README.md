# Counts of words and of sequences of words

The entry applies when a theorem states a property of a random draw as a count, without probability: the number of words with a property, `(Finset.univ.filter fun w : UInt64 => P w).card`, or of sequences of words, `(Finset.univ.filter fun ws : Fin M → UInt64 => P ws).card`.  A count over the 2^64 words, or over their sequences, gives the probability of the property for independent uniform words, and the proof needs only Mathlib's finite sets.  The random choice states its absence of bias in this form.

`Fintype UInt64` comes from `FinEnum UInt64` of `Mathlib.Data.FinEnum`, which a module that counts words imports, and `Fintype.card UInt64 = 2 ^ 64` follows from `FinEnum.card_eq_fintypeCard` and `FinEnum.card_UInt64`.  `Finset.card_bij` with `fun w _ => w.toNat` turns a count of words into a count of `Finset.range (2 ^ 64)`, as `Demos.RandomChoice.card_words` does, and `Nat.count_modEq_card` with `Nat.count_eq_card_filter_range` counts the residues below a bound, as `Demos.RandomChoice.residue_count` does.  A count of sequences whose condition holds coordinate by coordinate is the `Fintype.piFinset` of the coordinates' sets, and `Fintype.card_piFinset` gives the product, as `Demos.RandomChoice.picks_count` does.

A tail bound follows from an exponential moment in counting form.  `Fintype.prod_sum` turns the sum over sequences of `∏ i, f (ws i)` into `(∑ w, f w) ^ M`, and `Finset.prod_ite` with `Finset.prod_const` turns `∏ i, if p i then 1 else 3` into `3` to the number of coordinates where `p` fails, as `Demos.RandomChoice.moment_eq` does.  Every sequence in the counted set has a large exponent, so the count times that power of 3 is at most `(∑ w, f w) ^ M`, and `Demos.RandomChoice.short_count` bounds the sequences of `M` words with fewer than `K` accepted words by a `2^-512` part of all sequences.

Inequalities between powers with exponents in the hundreds fail with closed exponents: `decide` on `2 ^ 1536 ≤ 3 ^ 1025` reaches the recursion limit, `norm_num` and `ring` leave exponents above 256 unevaluated with a warning, and `ac_rfl` over a product with `2 ^ 512` reaches the recursion limit.  Prove the inequality with variable exponents, as `Demos.RandomChoice.pow_margin_gen` does, and instantiate it after rewriting the exponents with equations that `omega` proves, as `Demos.RandomChoice.pow_margin` does.  `rfl` on `Fintype.card UInt64` enumerates the type, so the cardinality comes from the `FinEnum` lemmas.

The worked examples are the theorems of `Demos/RandomChoice/Unbiased.lean`, whose module docstring states what the counts give for independent uniform words and what they leave out.
