# Cortex-M7 board theorems

The entry applies when `verified_compile` compiled the step `tick : G → UInt64 → G × UInt64` of a program with requests, the program runs through the Cortex-M7 back end, and the goal is a theorem for the STM32F767ZI microcontroller, an Arm Cortex-M7, on ST's NUCLEO-F767ZI development board, or for QEMU's `mps2-an500`.  The board theorem holds for every state that reset gives while the flash holds the image file, every sequence `σ` of the values that device reads return, and every number `N` of windows.  It states that the model reaches the loop's head of `main` with the trace `initTrace init σ ++ windows (reqTrace (devBus devBase) tick g0 …) N`, the initialization's accesses followed by those of the first `N` windows.  Its counted form bounds each window's steps, and a further form states that the run never stops.  [The entry on output of request programs](../request-output-prefix/README.md) states the windows' output in terms of a specification, and [the entry on image files](../image-file-checks/README.md) gives the check of the file that the theorem takes.

The step's closed facts come from the kernel by `decide +kernel`.  `LeanExe.M7.reachCovered m k = true` states that the functions that calls reach from the step have the forms that the simulation covers, `LeanExe.M7.usesArea m k` is `false` for a step that neither loads, stores, nor accesses a global, and `LeanExe.M7.stackBound m k` and `LeanExe.M7.staticCost m k = some c` give the stack's bound and a bound on the instructions of every run.  A step with an area adds the module's global types, `LeanExe.globalTypesOf m`, and `LeanExe.M7.setupOk m areaPages = true`, the fit of the module in the area that the setup writes.  `TestSupport.M7.echo_covered`, `echo_noArea`, `echo_stack`, and `echo_static` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/Step.lean`, `TestSupport.M7.crc_usesArea` and `crc_globals` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/CrcStep.lean`, and `TestSupport.M7.Crc.crc_setupOk` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/CrcLoop.lean` show each fact.

A library module whose functions a step will call is checked before the step exists.  `TestSupport.M7.CrcBitwise.functionsOf` runs the generator on the functions that calls reach from one function, laid out as `LeanExe.M7.genProgram` lays out a step's functions after `main` and with the shared addresses of a program without an area, and `TestSupport.M7.CrcBitwise.crcBitwise_functions` states by `decide +kernel` the size of the code that it emits.  `LeanExe.M7.usesArea`, `LeanExe.M7.reachCovered`, `LeanExe.M7.staticCost`, and `LeanExe.M7.stackBound` apply to any function, as `TestSupport.M7.CrcBitwise.crcBitwise_noArea`, `crcBitwise_covered`, `crcBitwise_static`, and `crcBitwise_stack` show, and `TestSupport.M7.CrcBitwise.crcBitwise_cost` applies `LeanExe.M7.costRun_le_staticCost` to every run of a call.  `staticCost` charges a call the callee's bound and the copies of its arguments and results, so the bounds of the library's functions enter the bound of the step that calls them.

For a step without an area, `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.board_run` takes the compiler's theorem `compiled.tick.implements` on `G × UInt64` with a precondition that always holds, the equations `hwords` and `hlen` between the state's `Scalar` values and its words, `reachCovered`, `usesArea m k = false`, the file check, and `8 * (s0.length + 1) + stackBound m k ≤ 0x20000`.  The heap and the memory cap at the module's initial store come from `Verified.Ready.initial rfl compiled.initial`, and `LeanExe.M7.boot_run`, which `board_run` applies, turns the compiler's theorem into the step's call fact `LeanExe.M7.StepCall` with the invariant `True` through `LeanExe.M7.stepCall_of_implementsA`.  `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.board_runs` gives the run that never stops from the same premises, as `TestSupport.M7.Nucleo.echo_runs` shows.  `TestSupport.M7.Nucleo.echo_loop` and `TestSupport.M7.Gw.board_loop` instantiate `board_run`.

A step whose state holds an `Int64` field takes the same theorem.  The state's `Flat` instance names the `Int64` as a component, as `Flat SgState (UInt64 × Int64 × UInt64)` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/Signed.lean` does, the instance `Flat Int64 UInt64` gives the field's word, and the word list names the field's `toUInt64`, as `TestSupport.M7.Signed.sgWords` does, so that `hwords` and `hlen` hold by `fun _ => rfl`.  The step's `Int64` order comparisons and `>>>` compile to `i64.lt_s`, `i64.le_s`, and `i64.shr_s`, which `LeanExe.M7.coveredInstr` admits and `LeanExe.M7.costRun_le_staticCost` bounds as straight-line forms.  `TestSupport.M7.Signed.board_loop`, `TestSupport.M7.Signed.board_loop_steps`, and `TestSupport.M7.Signed.qemu_loop` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/SignedLoop.lean` hold the board's and QEMU's loop theorems of one module's two steps in one file, and `TestSupport.M7.SignedBoardFile.sg_board` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/SignedBoardFile.lean` and `TestSupport.M7.SignedQemuFile.sg_qemu` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/SignedQemuFile.lean` apply them to the files.  The program list of `tests/TestSupport/M7/Windows.lean` needs an entry for each new step, as `TestSupport.M7.Windows.sgProg` gives, for `m7-windows static` and `m7-windows run`, and a step with a structure state needs its own `kind` function or `TestSupport.M7.Windows.echoKind` applied to its phase.

A step that loads from linear memory has an area, and its call fact needs an invariant that every call keeps besides the allocator's, such as `TestSupport.M7.Crc.CrcInv`, `heap.Aligned ∧ heap.Borrowed store 4096 crc32Table`.  The proof states the invariant at the module's initial store from `Verified.Ready.initial`, as `TestSupport.M7.Crc.crcInv_initial` does, derives a call fact from the compiler's `aligned` theorem under `set_option maxRecDepth 100000 in`, as `TestSupport.M7.Crc.crc_call` does, and packs it as `StepCall`, as `TestSupport.M7.crc_stepCall` does.  `LeanExe.heapAt_initStore` and `LeanExe.borrowed_initStore` carry the heap and the borrowed table to `initStore m areaPages`, as `TestSupport.M7.Crc.crc_initStore` does.  `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.areaBoard_run` then takes `StepCall`, `reachCovered`, `setupOk`, the heap and the invariant at `initStore m areaPages`, the file check, and the fit `areaSize areaPages + 8 * (s0.length + 1) + stackBound m k ≤ 0x20000`, and `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.areaBoard_runs` gives the run that never stops.

The board theorems take the initialization as a list of `InitOp`.  `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.boardWith_run`, `boardWith_run_steps`, `boardWith_runs`, `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.boardWith_setup`, `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.areaBoardWith_run`, `areaBoardWith_run_steps`, and `areaBoardWith_runs` take a list `ops` with the premise `∀ op ∈ ops, OpOk platform op`, the file check over the layout `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.bootWith` or `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.areaBootWith` of `ops`, and conclude with `initTrace ops σ` and `initReads ops`.  `board_run`, `areaBoard_run`, and the other names without `With` are their instances for `init`, so their statements name `init`.  The premise of a list of constant accesses holds by `decide`, since `LeanExe.M7.OpOk` is decidable, as `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.initOvrdis_ok` shows, and the list's confinement to RCC, GPIOD, and USART3 follows from `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.confined_of_addrs` and the list of its registers, which `rfl` proves, as `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.initOvrdis_addrs` and `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.initOvrdis_confined` show.  `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.initOvrdis` disables USART3's overrun detection, which a program needs when it must keep receiving after a byte arrives before it has read the one before, such as a framing that resynchronizes, and `TestSupport.M7.Sha.shaOvrdis_loop` applies `areaBoardWith_run` to the SHA-256 step with that list.  With `init`, one overrun stops USART3's reception until the next reset, as the trusted base's row on USART3 in `docs/m7-bare-metal.md` states.

`LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.board_run_steps` and `areaBoard_run_steps` state the counted run, `LeanExe.M7.Counted` with `LeanExe.M7.WindowBound`, and `LeanExe.M7.Counted.uniform` bounds each window by 16 steps of `main` plus the step's `staticCost`, as `TestSupport.M7.Nucleo.echo_loop_steps` applies it with `fun F st vs => costRun_le_staticCost rfl echo_static.1 F st vs`.  `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.init_confined` and `LeanExe.M7.Nucleo.windows_confined`, with a lemma that every request of the step names `USART_ISR`, `USART_RDR`, or `USART_TDR` such as `TestSupport.M7.Nucleo.usart3_requests`, confine the trace's accesses, as `TestSupport.M7.Nucleo.echo_confined` does.  `LeanExe.M7.step_call` states one call of the step from `StepCall` with the state's words in the outgoing area, as `TestSupport.M7.usart3_step` and `TestSupport.M7.crc_step` do, and `tests/baremetal/m7-image.sh` checks the code images whose instructions these step theorems describe.  On QEMU, `LeanExe.M7.boot_run` or `LeanExe.M7.setupBoot_run` takes the layout and platform of `tests/TestSupport/M7/Qemu.lean` and the facts of `tests/TestSupport/M7/QemuLoop.lean`, as `TestSupport.M7.Qemu.echo_loop`, `TestSupport.M7.Gw.qemu_loop`, and `TestSupport.M7.Crc.crcQemu_loop` do.

The board theorem lies in a file module that applies the loop theorem to the kernel's check of the file, as `gw_board` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/GwBoardFile.lean` applies `board_loop` to `gw_file`.  The run conditions of the header of `LeanExe/M7/Proof/Board.lean` are assumptions of every board theorem: a power-on reset, or an NRST reset with the debug registers at their power-on values, with BOOT0 low and BOOT_ADD0 `0x0080`, software watchdogs, and no interrupt, NMI, debug halt, debugger access, or DMA during the run.  The header of each board theorem's file module repeats them, and a new file module states them as well.

`staticCost` has no bound for a loop (header of `LeanExe/M7/Bound.lean`), so the SHA-256 step, whose `compress` loops, has no counted theorem, as the section "The final theorems" of `docs/m7-bare-metal.md` states.  No theorem bounds a window's time, which rests on cycle measurements (sections "Timing" and "Limits" of the same document).  The section "Limits" also records that a program with a form that no proof covers, such as `local.tee` or `unreachable`, fails the kernel's check of `reachCovered`, that the generator rejects floating point and a memory of more than one page, and that a step that allocates has no board theorem, since its compiler theorem requires room in the heap that unboundedly many windows need not leave.  The steps of the worked examples therefore compile with `verified_compile (pages := 1) (cap := 1)`, as `tests/TestSupport/M7/Crc.lean` shows.

The worked examples are `TestSupport.M7.Nucleo.echo_loop` and `echo_loop_steps` for a step without an area, `TestSupport.M7.Gw.board_loop` and `board_loop_steps` for the gateway, and `TestSupport.M7.Crc.crc_loop` and `TestSupport.M7.Sha.sha_loop` for steps with an area.  Their file modules, which read the images and lie outside the build, hold `echo_board` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/NucleoFile.lean`, `gw_board_steps` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/GwBoardFile.lean`, `crc_board` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/CrcBoardFile.lean`, and `sha_board` of `tests/TestSupport/M7/ShaBoardFile.lean`.  `tests/baremetal/m7-board.sh` elaborates them in the directory of the images, and `tests/baremetal/m7-qemu.sh` does the same for the QEMU file modules, such as `tests/TestSupport/M7/QemuFile.lean`.
