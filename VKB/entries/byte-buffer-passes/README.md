# Byte buffers in words and straight-line passes

The entry applies when a step for the Cortex-M7 back end holds a buffer of bytes in its state and passes over the whole buffer in one window, as a decoder or an encoder of a record does, and the step needs a counted board theorem.  `LeanExe.M7.staticCost` bounds only code without loops or recursion (header of `LeanExe/M7/Bound.lean`), so the pass must run as a fixed sequence of calls over a fixed number of words.  The proof relates the words to a list of bytes and the sequence of calls to a fold over that list, and a lemma about lists states the specification as the same fold.

`LeanExe.Requests.Bytes40` holds 40 bytes in five words, the first byte the most significant byte of `w0`, and `LeanExe.Requests.Bytes40.bytes` lists them.  `LeanExe.Requests.push40` moves every byte one place toward the start and puts a byte at the end, and `LeanExe.Requests.unshift40` moves every byte one place toward the end and puts a byte at the start, each with five shifts and five additions and no test of a position.  `LeanExe.Requests.push40_bytes` and `LeanExe.Requests.unshift40_bytes` state them on the list as `b.bytes.tail ++ [x.toUInt8]` and `x.toUInt8 :: b.bytes.dropLast`, and `LeanExe.Requests.first40_eq` and `LeanExe.Requests.last40_eq` read the first and the last byte.  A buffer that receives a segment with `push40` holds the segment's bytes at its end, so an abstraction takes `b.bytes.drop (40 - len)`, and a buffer that a writer empties from the start holds the bytes left to write at its start, so an abstraction takes `b.bytes.take k`, as `TestSupport.M7.Delim.dlAbs` does with both.  [The entry on word identities](../word-bit-identities/README.md) gives the method of the byte lemmas.

A pass calls one function per position, written as nested calls: `LeanExe.Requests.dec8` applies `LeanExe.Requests.decPos` eight times and `LeanExe.Requests.decAll` applies `dec8` five times, and `LeanExe.Requests.enc8` and `LeanExe.Requests.encAll` do the same with `LeanExe.Requests.encPos`.  A position of the decoder takes the first byte of the source buffer with `first40` and moves the buffer with `push40`, and a position of the encoder takes the last byte with `last40` and moves the buffer with `unshift40`, so the calls of a pass run the same code.  A count in the state makes the decoder skip the positions before the segment and the encoder leave its state unchanged at the positions after the record.  The encoder runs from the record's last byte back, because a COBS code byte is the distance to the next zero, which the bytes after it determine.  `TestSupport.M7.dl_static` and `TestSupport.M7.dl_stack` state the static cost and the stack bound of a step whose window runs both passes.

The proof states each pass as an iterate.  `LeanExe.Requests.iterate_eight` and `LeanExe.Requests.iterate_five` hold by `rfl` for every function `f`, and `LeanExe.Requests.dec8_iter` and `LeanExe.Requests.decAll_iter` rewrite the nested calls as `decPos^[40]` with them and `Function.iterate_mul`, as `LeanExe.Requests.enc8_iter` and `LeanExe.Requests.encAll_iter` do for the encoder.  A proof of `decAll d = decPos^[40] d` by `rfl` reached the recursion limit, and a proof of `dec8 = decPos^[8]` by `funext` and `rfl` timed out at `whnf` with `maxRecDepth` raised to 100,000, while `rw [iterate_eight]` before the `rfl` closes it at the default limits.  `LeanExe.Requests.decPos_iter` proves by induction on `j` that `decPos^[j]` relates, through `LeanExe.Requests.DecRel`, the words to the left fold of `LeanExe.Requests.decStepL` over the first `j` positions after the skipped ones, and `LeanExe.Requests.encPos_iter` relates `encPos^[j]`, through `LeanExe.Requests.EncRel`, to the right fold `LeanExe.Requests.encBack` over the last bytes.  `LeanExe.Requests.cobsDecode_fold` states `cobsDecode` as `LeanExe.Requests.decFinish` of the left fold, and `LeanExe.Requests.cobsEncode_back` states `cobsEncode` of a record shorter than 254 bytes through the right fold, so `LeanExe.Requests.decodeWords_eq`, `LeanExe.Requests.encAll_spec`, `LeanExe.Requests.encByte_spec`, and `LeanExe.Requests.encFinish_spec` give the passes in terms of the specification.  [The entry on loops and builds](../loop-combinators/README.md) relates a chain of conditional steps to a loop in the same way.

`verified_compile` timed out at `whnf` after 200,000 heartbeats on `encByte (encByte (encAll (encStart src n)) (c &&& 255)) code`, in which `encByte` takes the call of `encAll` as its argument, for a cause not located.  `encAll (encStart src n)` alone compiled, and the same expression compiled with the result of `encAll` named by `let` before `encByte` takes it, as `TestSupport.M7.Delim.dlAnswer` does.  An `if` that chose between calls of `encByte` on small arguments compiled as well.  The command in `tests/TestSupport/M7/Delim.lean` raises `maxRecDepth` to 100,000, since at the default limit it stopped with `maximum recursion depth has been reached` on a version of the step with the same passes.

A step whose machine reads fixed fields of a decoded record takes them from the decoder's output buffer, whose last `olen` bytes hold the record, so a record of 35 bytes lies at `out.bytes.drop 5` and each field is a combination of shifted and masked words, as `Demos.Bms.Program.decodeRec` reads them and `Demos.Bms.Program.decodeRec_spec` proves.  An answer of fixed length that the step builds in words goes at the end of a buffer, so that `LeanExe.Requests.encStart` with its length encodes it, as `Demos.Bms.Program.answerSrc` lays out 17 bytes from position 23.  `LeanExe.Requests.seg_store`, `LeanExe.Requests.headD_take`, `LeanExe.Requests.tail_take_push`, `LeanExe.Requests.first40_val`, and `LeanExe.Requests.answer_rel` of `LeanExe/Requests/DelimitedSteps.lean` give the abstraction's equations for a store of a byte, a write of the answer's first byte, and the encoded answer, for any step with segments of at most 36 bytes.

The worked examples are the echo of records and the BMS step.  `TestSupport.M7.Delim.dlAnswer_spec` states the answer window's buffer as the encoded answer from `decodeWords_eq`, `encAll_spec`, `encByte_spec`, and `encFinish_spec`, and `TestSupport.M7.Delim.dlPhases_abs` states the whole step under `TestSupport.M7.Delim.dlAbs`.  `Demos.Bms.Program.bmsDelim_spec` and `Demos.Bms.Program.bmsPhases_abs` do the same for a step that dispatches the window of a delimiter to its own function.  [The entry on output of request programs](../request-output-prefix/README.md) carries that abstraction to the bytes that the windows write.  `TestSupport.M7.Delim.board_loop_steps` bounds each window by the cost of the window that runs both passes.
