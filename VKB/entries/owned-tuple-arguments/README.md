# Owned tuple parameters and their theorem arguments

The entry applies when a function of the verified compiler takes a tuple or structure parameter owned and a proof states its theorem or bounds the charge of a call of it.  The theorem of such a function takes the argument as `Moved` of the parameter's own Lean type, `Moved (α × β)` or `Moved S`, through the instances of `LeanExe/Pipeline/MovedTuples.lean`.  A moved pair states in `borrowed` that the objects of its components lie apart, since `Verified.ImplementsA.comap` converts an argument from `LeanExe.Pipeline.Represent.borrowed` alone, and it moves every pointer of both components.  Lean's synthesis gives a pair without heap data inside a moved pair the instance from `Scalar`, whose priority `low` lies above the moved pair's 50, so `Verified.Ty.movedInst` gives such a pair that instance as well and `Verified.Agree.movedScalar` meets it.  `Verified.Reflect.agreeMoved` recurs on the source type, and `Verified.Examples.OwnedTuples` checks the statement for functions written in the source language.

A process step takes its state owned and moves it into a helper whose tuple parameter is owned, so the call copies nothing.  When the helper's parameter is paid, the call also pays the credits of the state's arrays, and the helper's generated bound is those credits plus its cost, as `Demos.RunningSum.inputCharge` and `Demos.OrderBook.Process.readCharge` show.  After `simp` with `Verified.Expr.ownedCost`, `Verified.Var.ownedCost`, `Verified.modeAt`, `Verified.Wasi.Resource.letPairModes`, `Verified.Mode.join`, the lemmas `Verified.Args.get_cons_zero'` and `Verified.Args.get_cons_succ`, and `Verified.Var.index_ofIndex`, such a call's charge has the form `credit + cost`, which a `show` with `sourceState` restates, as `Demos.RunningSum.step_body_bounded` and `Demos.OrderBook.Process.readStep_bounded` do.  [The entry on source forms and costs](../verified-source-costs/README.md) gives the rule by which the reflector makes a parameter owned.
