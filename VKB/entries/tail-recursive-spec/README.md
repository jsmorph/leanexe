# Specifications of tail-recursive and fuel-bounded functions

Besides `LeanExe.loop`, a dialect program recurses through a definition by well-founded recursion, whose tail calls the compiler translates into a loop, through a recursion on an explicit fuel argument that makes the function total, or through `LeanExe.repeatWhile fuel init cond step`.  `LeanExe.Dialect.loop_induction` does not apply to these, so the specification theorem needs a recursion or an induction of its own.  The theorem states an invariant of the arguments and the property of the result that the invariant gives where the recursion stops.

For well-founded recursion, prove the property by a theorem that recurses with the program's own `termination_by` and `decreasing_by`, and follow the program's branches with `rw [f]` and `ite_eq_left` or `ite_eq_right`.  `Library.Arrays.BinarySearch.search_spec` carries the invariant that every element before `lo` is below the key and every element from `hi` on is at least the key, with `termination_by (hi - lo).toNat` as `search` has it, and heapsort's sift does the same (`heap-sift-invariant`).  `fun_induction f args` instead gives one case for each branch of the definition, with the induction hypotheses of its recursive calls, as `Library.Sorting.Quicksort.stages_perm`, `Library.Sorting.Shellsort.passes_perm`, and `Library.Sorting.Find.find_spec` use it.  State such a theorem as an implication over all the arguments, such as `e.toNat ≤ a.size → …`, so that each case's hypothesis applies at the arguments of the recursive call.

For a fuel argument, state the lemma for every fuel at least a measure of the remaining work, and induct on a natural number.  `Library.Graphs.UnionFind.root_iter` states that `root p x F` returns the root `d` links above `x` for every `F` with `d ≤ F.toNat`, by induction on `d`, and `root_eq` applies it at the depth of the root.  `Library.Strings.Kmp.fall_spec` takes the fuel's value `f` with `k.toNat ≤ f` and inducts on `f`, and `Library.HashTables.LinearProbing.probe_spec` and `close_spec` tie the fuel to the remaining distance, as `fuel.toNat = M.toNat - d` does in `probe_spec`, and recurse on that distance with `termination_by`.  When the result's properties hold whatever the fuel, the lemma needs no bound: `Library.Graphs.WeightedUnionFind.compress_spec` holds for every fuel, by induction on its value.

For `LeanExe.repeatWhile`, `Library.Combinatorics.DancingLinks.repeatWhile_inv` gives an invariant `P` and the failed condition at the end from `P init`, a measure with `μ init < fuel.toNat`, and a step that keeps `P` and lowers the measure while the condition holds.  `repeatWhile_of_inv` adds a property `Q` that the invariant gives where the condition fails, and the traversals of dancing links, such as `Library.Combinatorics.DancingLinks.hideColumn_eq`, apply it with the fuel `t.size.toUInt64`.  The demos carry variants: `Demos.Euler.repeatWhile_inv` and `Demos.Slew.Search.repeatWhile_induction` keep an invariant without a measure, and `Demos.OrderBook.Refinement.repeatWhile_inv` takes `μ init ≤ fuel.toNat`.  One lemma in the dialect would replace the copies.

A `let` before a recursive call becomes a local definition in the cases of `fun_induction`.  The cases of `stages_perm` name the partition's result `r`, and each states the partition's facts with `have hp : … r.1 … := part_within l e a hc.1 he`, which elaboration accepts by unfolding the local.  The program's own `decreasing_by` rewrites with `simp only [r] at *`, and the compiler's termination proof of the tail loop meets the same local definitions, which `tail-termination-cases` covers.

The worked examples are `Library.Arrays.BinarySearch.search_spec` and `lowerBound_eq`, `Library.Sorting.Quicksort.stages_perm`, `Library.Graphs.UnionFind.root_eq`, `Library.Graphs.WeightedUnionFind.compress_spec`, `Library.Strings.Kmp.fall_spec`, `Library.HashTables.LinearProbing.probe_spec`, and `Library.Combinatorics.DancingLinks.count_eq`.  `Library.Graphs.UnionFind.classes_spec` and `Library.Graphs.WeightedUnionFind.classes_spec` join `root_eq` with a loop invariant, `Library.Graphs.UnionFind.Inv`, which `loop-combinators` describes.  `count_eq` composes the dancing-links search, a recursion on the depth whose traversals are `repeatWhile` loops, with the specification's count of exact covers.
