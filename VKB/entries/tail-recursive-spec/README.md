# Specifications of tail-recursive and fuel-bounded functions

Besides `LeanExe.loop`, a dialect program recurses through a definition by well-founded recursion, whose tail calls the compiler translates into a loop, through a recursion on an explicit fuel argument that makes the function total, or through `LeanExe.repeatWhile fuel init cond step`.  `LeanExe.Dialect.loop_induction` does not apply to these, so the specification theorem needs a recursion or an induction of its own.  The theorem states an invariant of the arguments and the property of the result that the invariant gives where the recursion stops.

For well-founded recursion, prove the property by a theorem that recurses with the program's own `termination_by` and `decreasing_by`, and follow the program's branches with `rw [f]` and `ite_eq_left` or `ite_eq_right`.  `Library.Arrays.BinarySearch.search_spec` carries the invariant that every element before `lo` is below the key and every element from `hi` on is at least the key, with `termination_by (hi - lo).toNat` as `search` has it, and heapsort's sift does the same ([the entry on array heaps](../heap-sift-invariant/README.md)).  `fun_induction f args` instead gives one case for each branch of the definition, with the induction hypotheses of its recursive calls, as `Library.Sorting.Quicksort.stages_perm`, `Library.Sorting.Shellsort.passes_perm`, and `Library.Sorting.Find.find_spec` use it.  State such a theorem as an implication over all the arguments, such as `e.toNat ≤ a.size → …`, so that each case's hypothesis applies at the arguments of the recursive call.

For a fuel argument, state the lemma for every fuel at least a measure of the remaining work, and induct on a natural number.  `Library.Graphs.UnionFind.root_iter` states that `root p x F` returns the root `d` links above `x` for every `F` with `d ≤ F.toNat`, by induction on `d`, and `root_eq` applies it at the depth of the root.  `Library.Strings.Kmp.fall_spec` takes the fuel's value `f` with `k.toNat ≤ f` and inducts on `f`, and `Library.HashTables.LinearProbing.probe_spec` and `close_spec` tie the fuel to the remaining distance, as `fuel.toNat = M.toNat - d` does in `probe_spec`, and recurse on that distance with `termination_by`.  When the result's properties hold whatever the fuel, the lemma needs no bound: `Library.Graphs.WeightedUnionFind.compress_spec` holds for every fuel, by induction on its value.  A function that returns the rounds of its run, whose theorems concern the rounds rather than one result, is better served by an inductive trace of the run that no lemma states with a fuel, as [the entry on traces of fuel-bounded runs](../run-traces/README.md) describes.

For `LeanExe.repeatWhile`, `Library.Combinatorics.DancingLinks.repeatWhile_inv` gives an invariant `P` and the failed condition at the end from `P init`, a measure with `μ init < fuel.toNat`, and a step that keeps `P` and lowers the measure while the condition holds.  `repeatWhile_of_inv` adds a property `Q` that the invariant gives where the condition fails, and the traversals of dancing links, such as `Library.Combinatorics.DancingLinks.hideColumn_eq`, apply it with the fuel `t.size.toUInt64`.  The demos carry variants: `Demos.Euler.repeatWhile_inv` and `Demos.Slew.Search.repeatWhile_induction` keep an invariant without a measure, and `Demos.OrderBook.Refinement.repeatWhile_inv` takes `μ init ≤ fuel.toNat`.  One lemma in the dialect would replace the copies.

A `let` before a recursive call becomes a local definition in the cases of `fun_induction`.  The cases of `stages_perm` name the partition's result `r`, and each states the partition's facts with `have hp : … r.1 … := part_within l e a hc.1 he`, which elaboration accepts by unfolding the local.  The program's own `decreasing_by` rewrites with `simp only [r] at *`, and the compiler's termination proof of the tail loop meets the same local definitions, which [the entry on tail-termination cases](../tail-termination-cases/README.md) covers.

A specification may decide at an element by reading later elements while the program scans once and keeps a small count of what it has passed, as the Maine interpretation ends a ballot at an empty position when the next position is empty and a later one is marked.  An invariant that relates the program's state to the specification's examination of the remaining elements alone fails, because the program has already passed the elements that the specification reads ahead.  State the program's result from position `p` with the count `g` as the specification's result on `g` neutral elements followed by the remaining ones, as `Demos.Rcv.Maine.Refine.examineAt_eq` does with `List.replicate g.toNat 0 ++ wordsAt ws p.toNat m`.  The induction on the number of remaining elements then needs equations of the specification on short prefixes that bound the count: two zero words before a marked one end the examination (`Demos.Rcv.Maine.Refine.examine_gap_two`), fewer change nothing before a marked one (`Demos.Rcv.Maine.Refine.examine_gap_ne`), and a third changes nothing (`Demos.Rcv.Maine.Refine.examine_three_zeros`), so a count capped at 2 suffices.  The inductive step splits on `g = 0 ∨ g = 1 ∨ g = 2` through `UInt64.toNat_inj` and rewrites the program's `if g == 0 then 1 else 2` at each literal with `show … from rfl` before it applies the induction hypothesis, and `Demos.Rcv.Maine.Refine.examineAt_interpret` takes the result at `g = 0`.

A recursion that the compiler does not translate into a loop takes the call depth, and its generated `trapFree` theorem assumes the frames condition `p.f.fits 1000 x`, which states that every call of the run to such a function happens below depth 1,000 ([the manual's section on bounds of recursive functions](../../../docs/verified-manual.md#bounds-of-recursive-functions)).  The specification's precondition must then keep the depth below that limit.  In dancing links, `count` calls `search` at depth 1 and the search at level `l` runs at depth `l + 1`, so `Library.Combinatorics.DancingLinks.itemLimit` admits at most 998 items (its docstring in `Library/Combinatorics/DancingLinks/Program.lean`).  `Library.Combinatorics.DancingLinks.search_fits` proves the frames condition by induction on the depth bound, `Library.Combinatorics.DancingLinks.count_fits` derives it for a valid input from that limit, and `Library.Combinatorics.DancingLinks.count_call` composes the generated `trapFree` with `count_eq` (header of `Library/Combinatorics/DancingLinks/Compiled.lean`).

The worked examples are `Library.Arrays.BinarySearch.search_spec` and `lowerBound_eq`, `Library.Sorting.Quicksort.stages_perm`, `Library.Graphs.UnionFind.root_eq`, `Library.Graphs.WeightedUnionFind.compress_spec`, `Library.Strings.Kmp.fall_spec`, `Library.HashTables.LinearProbing.probe_spec`, and `Library.Combinatorics.DancingLinks.count_eq`.  `Library.Graphs.UnionFind.classes_spec` and `Library.Graphs.WeightedUnionFind.classes_spec` join `root_eq` with a loop invariant, `Library.Graphs.UnionFind.Inv`, which [the entry on loops and builds](../loop-combinators/README.md) describes.  `count_eq` composes the dancing-links search, a recursion on the depth whose traversals are `repeatWhile` loops, with the specification's count of exact covers.
