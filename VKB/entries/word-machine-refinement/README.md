# A machine over words against its specification over numbers

The entry applies when a record machine specified over natural numbers, integers, lists, and structures, such as `Demos.Bms.Spec.step`, runs in a compiled step as a program over `UInt64` words in structures with `Flat` instances, and the goal is that the program's step equals the specification's under an abstraction, so that the specification's theorems hold of the compiled program.  The program reads its input through a decoder, compares readings with limits, and keeps counters, flags, and a protection state in words, while the specification keeps unbounded numbers, `Bool` fields, and an inductive state.  The proof's work lies in many small equations, one for each check, field, and transition, each of which a tactic proves over `toNat`.

Map the words to the specification's state with a function such as `Demos.Bms.Program.absS`, the flags through `== 1` and the counters through `toNat`, and bound the words with an invariant such as `Demos.Bms.Program.StInv` as the specification's values stay bounded: flags 0 or 1, sequence numbers below `2 ^ 32`, ages up to their cap, and a precharge count below its limit, so that a word's arithmetic does not wrap.  Write the program function by function after the specification, `Demos.Bms.Program.failW` after `Demos.Bms.Spec.failure` and `Demos.Bms.Program.rule5W` after `Demos.Bms.Spec.rule5`, so that each lemma relates one pair: `Demos.Bms.Program.rule5W_spec`, `Demos.Bms.Program.nextW_spec`, and `Demos.Bms.Program.updW_spec`.  Write each check as a `Bool` function of the program and prove an equation of `Bool`s with the specification's check, such as `Demos.Bms.Program.sensor_eq` and `Demos.Bms.Program.aux_eq`: `Bool.eq_iff_iff` turns it into an equivalence, `simp` with `decide_eq_true_eq`, `UInt64.lt_iff_toNat_lt`, `UInt64.toNat_add`, `Demos.Bms.Program.bitW_toNat`, `Nat.testBit_eq_decide_div_mod_eq`, `Demos.Bms.Program.natAbs_le_iff`, and `Demos.Bms.Program.lt_natAbs_iff` turns its sides into arithmetic, the profile's constants unfold last, and `omega` closes it, as the macro `flag_eq` of `Demos/Bms/Abs.lean` does.

A signed reading compares with negative limits as a word once both are biased: `Demos.Bms.Program.bias16` adds `2 ^ 15` modulo `2 ^ 16`, and `Demos.Bms.Program.signed16_eq` states `Demos.Bms.Spec.signed` of the field as the biased word less `2 ^ 15`, so `omega` decides every comparison.  A specification that returns the first failing check of a list, `Demos.Bms.Spec.firstFailure`, meets the program's chain of `if`s through `Demos.Bms.Program.firstFailure_cons` and `Demos.Bms.Program.optCode_ite`, after which the two sides agree by `rfl`, as `Demos.Bms.Program.contentW_spec` shows.  A program that sees its input through a decoder takes a relation between the decoder's result and the specification's input, `Demos.Bms.Program.ViewOf`, which one lemma establishes for the decoder, `Demos.Bms.Program.view_decode`, and which the machine's lemmas take as a premise, so they hold for every input.  Fields that the program reads from a buffer's words equal `Demos.Bms.Spec.field` of the buffer's bytes once both sides become explicit lists of `(w >>> s).toUInt8`, as `Demos.Bms.Program.decodeRec_spec` and `Demos.Bms.Program.answerSrc_spec` show with the macro `byte_eq`.  A window theorem then states the program's result with existentials closed by `rfl`, `Demos.Bms.Program.bmsDelim_spec`, and the step's commutation with the framing follows the pattern of [the entry on byte buffers in words](../byte-buffer-passes/README.md), `Demos.Bms.Program.bmsPhases_abs`.

`simp` that unfolds a constant such as `Demos.Bms.Spec.vMax` inside the proposition of a `decide` rewrites the proposition and leaves the instance with the old one, and a later `simp` stops with "Application type mismatch", so eliminate `decide` before unfolding constants.  For the same reason unfold the abstraction, `Demos.Bms.Program.absS`, before the definition whose body holds the `decide`, such as `Demos.Bms.Spec.auxFault`.  `trivial` among the closers of byte equations tried `decide` on terms with free `UInt64` variables and timed out at `whnf`, an error that Lean reports at the declaration, and `exact True.intro` replaced it.  `congr` on the CRC of two lists of bytes timed out in the same way, and `congrArg` replaced it.  `decide +kernel` on `Library.Hashing.Crc.Division.crc32Eval` of a link identifier of 16 bytes timed out at the elaborator's `whnf`, and `Demos.Bms.Program.linkCrc_spec` takes the CRC from `Library.Hashing.Crc.Proof.crc32_spec` of the table program over the bytes packed in two words, which the kernel decides at once.  The simproc `reduceToNat` of `UInt64` turns `min (3 : UInt64).toNat 8` into `min 3 8`, which blocks the unfolding of `LeanExe.Requests.wordBytes`, so rewrite it with a lemma proved by `rfl` first, such as `Demos.Bms.Program.min3`.

The worked example is the BMS step of `Demos/Bms/Program.lean` and its proof in `Demos/Bms/Abs.lean`, from `Demos.Bms.Program.decodeRec_spec` and `Demos.Bms.Program.recCrc_spec` through `Demos.Bms.Program.failW_spec`, `Demos.Bms.Program.updW_spec`, and `Demos.Bms.Program.answer_eq` to `Demos.Bms.Program.bmsPhases_abs`.
