# Floating-point equations from Sail's Lean translation

The Isla theorem of an A64 floating-point form takes as a hypothesis the equation of the Arm function that its trace leaves abstract, such as `LeanExe.A64.Isla.FPAddEq A`: the meaning `A` of the wrapper `FPAdd64` returns Talos's `Wasm.f64Add` of the operands' words under FPCR `0x02000000`.  Isla does not evaluate the pseudocode's arithmetic on real numbers, so the traces give no proof of the equation, and the modules of `tools/sail/F7` prove it over Sail's Lean translation of the function (`LeanExe/A64/Isla/Float.lean`, header).  The entry applies when a form gains such an equation, when a proof of `tools/sail/F7` fails, and after a change that those proofs depend on.

A new floating-point form needs a wrapper in `tests/isla/armv9p4-checked-fp.diff`, its equation in `LeanExe/A64/Isla/Float.lean`, a theorem in `tools/sail/F7` that the translated function returns Talos's function from every state that satisfies `F7.Hyp`, as `F7.fpAdd_eq_f64Add` of `F7/FPAdd.lean` does, and a link and a derivation in `F7/Isla.lean` or `F7/Isla32.lean`, such as `F7.SailFPAdd` and `F7.fpAddEq_of_sail`.  The new theorems' names go in `tools/sail/Axioms.lean`, whose axiom report the check reads.  Lean does not check the link: it states that Isla's abstract call returns what the translation returns from a state that satisfies `Hyp`, and it rests on the review that [the Sail README's section on the join with the Isla equations](../../../tools/sail/README.md#the-join-with-the-isla-equations) describes.  The `F7` names live in tracked files that the repository's build does not compile, so this entry names them in prose and lists only the Isla equations in `entry.json`.

`tools/sail/check.sh` runs on a host with Sail 0.20.3 in the opam switch `sail-0.20.3`, outside the repository's build.  It regenerates the translation from pinned inputs, compares the hashes of the inputs and of the generated and edited files with `tools/sail/check.sha256`, builds the library `F7` in a scratch copy of the repository twice, once with `undefined_real` returning an opaque constant and once as generated, and fails on any axiom beyond `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.  `F7/Base.lean` imports Talos's `Interpreter.Wasm.Float`, and `F7/Isla.lean` imports `LeanExe.A64.Isla.Float`, so a change of either, of Mathlib, or of the toolchain needs a run of `check.sh`, which no step of the full check performs.  [The Sail README's section on the check](../../../tools/sail/README.md#the-check) gives the command and the runs' measurements with their CPU quotas.

The Sail README records each kind of lesson in its own section, with the measurements and configurations that this entry does not copy.  [Translation](../../../tools/sail/README.md#translation) and [Elaboration](../../../tools/sail/README.md#elaboration) cover the slice of the model, the edits of `fix-output.sh`, the removed coercion of `Int` to `Nat`, and the comparisons that show each edit changes nothing else.  [Loops](../../../tools/sail/README.md#loops) covers the fuel of `whileFuelM`, which returns its current value when the fuel runs out, and the proofs that both loops exit within it.  [The state hypothesis](../../../tools/sail/README.md#the-state-hypothesis) covers what `Hyp` assumes and the operands of `&&` and `||` that the translation evaluates eagerly, and [Unspecified values](../../../tools/sail/README.md#unspecified-values) covers the choice source and the opaque real.  [The proofs](../../../tools/sail/README.md#the-proofs) and [The binary32 proofs](../../../tools/sail/README.md#the-binary32-proofs) cover the rounding cores, Talos's rounders, and the kernel's evaluation of words that hold `2 ^ 1074` under the different `Pow` instances of Lean's core and Mathlib, which [the entry on kernel evaluation costs](../kernel-evaluation-costs/README.md) generalizes.  [What the proofs trust](../../../tools/sail/README.md#what-the-proofs-trust) lists what the theorems of `F7` assume of Sail, lean-sail, the edits, and the links.

`lake env lean` runs `lean` with Lake's search paths but without a library's `moreLeanArgs`, which give the translation's libraries `--tstack=400000`.  A file-mode run without the flag stopped with "Stack overflow detected", so `tools/sail/lean-file.sh` and the axiom step of `check.sh` pass `--tstack=400000` themselves.  A new script that elaborates a file of the translation or of `F7` with `lake env lean` needs the same flag.

The worked example is `fadd_tcg` of `tests/TestSupport/A64/Isla/Float.lean`, which takes `hA : FPAddEq A`, together with `F7.fpAddEq_of_sail` of `tools/sail/F7/Isla.lean`, which derives that hypothesis from `F7.SailFPAdd A` and `F7.fpAdd_eq_f64Add`.  `F7.fpCompareEq_of_sail` derives `LeanExe.A64.Isla.FPCompareEq` for FCMP, which calls FPCompare without a wrapper, and `F7/Isla32.lean` derives the binary32 equations, among them `LeanExe.A64.Isla.FPAdd32Eq` and `LeanExe.A64.Isla.FPRoundInt32Eq`.  [The entry on instruction forms against Isla](../isla-form-proofs/README.md) describes the theorems that take these hypotheses.
