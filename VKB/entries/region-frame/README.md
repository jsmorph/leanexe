# Region frame of a call or a step

The entry applies when a proof needs the caller's arrays, buffers, or trees after a call of a compiled function, or composes steps that allocate, release, or write.  `ImplementsA`, `Verified.ImplementsTables`, and `Verified.ImplementsStatic`, the statements of `p.f.implements` and `p.f.trapFree`, end with the same region clause.  Every region of the heap before the call that lies below `top` and outside every free block (`Heap.Region`), has positive length, and lies apart from the consumed blocks (`Apart`) keeps its bytes, remains such a region of the final heap, and lies apart from the blocks of the result (`Represent.outside`).

The guard `0 < r.2` excludes empty regions.  An empty region on the boundary between a released block and a free block is a region before a release that merges the two blocks, and not after it.  The regions that the proofs keep have positive length: an array's words because of its length word, an object's block because of its header, and a record's slots when the record has a slot.

Inside proofs the clause takes the form `Heap.Keeps heap initial gone heap' store fresh`, with the consumed blocks in `gone` and the new blocks in `fresh`.  `Heap.Keeps.implements` converts the clause of a theorem to this form, with `gone` the blocks of the moved pointers and `fresh` the result's blocks, and `apart_blocks` converts `Apart` over pointers to apartness from their blocks.  `Heap.Keeps.refl` starts a chain, `Heap.Keeps.trans` and `Heap.Keeps.transBoth` compose two steps when the second consumes only blocks that the first leaves fresh or consumes, and `Heap.Keeps.mono` changes the lists.  `Heap.Keeps.release` is the frame of a release, which `Verified.releaseStep` uses.

`Heap.Keeps.borrowed` and `Heap.Keeps.owned` give the caller's arrays back from a frame, with the same contents and, for an owned array, the same capacity.  They rest on `Heap.Borrowed.keepIn` and `Heap.Owned.keepIn`, which need the kept bytes, the region in the new heap, and `Heap.At` of the new store, and no bound on pages.  `LeanExe.ProofKit.BorrowedKept.borrowed_kept` applies `keepIn` to the region clause directly, and `Verified.ImplementsTables.call` uses it to keep every array borrowed before a call, the program's tables among them.

`Heap.Keeps.node` gives an owned value of a recursive type back with the same blocks, and `Heap.Keeps.nodeBorrowed` a borrowed one, whose slot regions (`Node.slotRegions`) must have positive length and lie apart from the consumed blocks.  The slot regions are each record's slots, with the length word and elements of each array and the block of each buffer that the records hold.  `Node.slotRegions_pos` gives the positive lengths for a value whose every record has a slot (`Node.Slotted`), and `EncodeSlotted.slotRegions_pos` for a type with an `EncodeSlotted` instance, which `List UInt64`, `Words`, `KeyTree`, and the verified compiler's `Verified.Tree shape` have.  `NodeBorrowed.regions` gives each slot region as a `Heap.Region`, and `NodeBorrowed.frame` keeps the value when its slot regions keep their bytes and remain regions.

The premise `Separate` of `ImplementsA` keeps the consumed blocks pairwise apart and apart from `Represent.reads`: an array argument's words, the block of a buffer argument whose root is nonzero, and a borrowed tree argument's slot regions.  A borrowed value passed beside a consumed one therefore starts apart from it, and the region clause keeps the borrowed value's regions through the call.  A proof that composes two calls by hand converts both clauses with `Heap.Keeps.implements`, composes them with `Heap.Keeps.trans`, and reads the values back with the keep lemmas above.

The verified compiler's own proofs carry the frame in `Verified.Step heap store keep heap' store' fresh` of `Verified/HeapStep.lean`.  `Step` adds the allocator invariant and the unchanged caps and names the kept regions by a predicate `keep` instead of a list of consumed blocks.  `Step.refl`, `Step.trans`, `Step.transBoth`, and `Step.mono` compose steps, `Step.allocate` is an allocation, and `Step.borrowed` and `Step.owned` give an array back through `keepIn`.  `Verified.Step.nodeBorrowed` keeps a borrowed tree through a step that keeps every region, from `NodeBorrowed.regions`, `Node.slotRegions_pos`, and `NodeBorrowed.frame`.
