# Diagnosing slow checks

The entry applies when a Lean check takes much longer than comparable checks, exhausts its memory, heartbeats, or recursion depth, or stops at a timeout without a diagnostic, and its cause is not yet known.  It gives the order of the measurements, the observations that separate the elaborator's work from the kernel's check and from compiled code, and the causes found in this repository with their remedies, each linked to the entry that holds its details and its worked examples.  [The entry on measuring Lean checks](../measuring-lean-checks/README.md) states how to take each measurement and how to compare two of them.

1. Observe the job before changing the proof.  Run the check through `tools/leanrun` with a memory cap and a timeout, and after a timeout without a diagnostic divide the proof or the module before running it again ([the repository instructions on Lean process limits](../../../AGENTS.md#lean-process-limits)).  Record the wall and processor time, the scope's `memory.peak` and its count of `high` events, and for a single process its `RssAnon`, since its resident set also counts the mapped `.olean` files of the imports ([proc_pid_status(5)](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man5/proc_pid_status.5.html)).  A job held at its `MemoryHigh` with a small share of a processor is throttled, and its time then measures the limit, so run it alone, with fewer threads, or with a higher limit before diagnosing the proof, as the section "The full check" of `docs/verified-manual.md` records for `Demos/OrderBook/ProcessCharges.lean`.

2. Find the declaration.  A probe file that holds the module's imports and one declaration, run under a short timeout, measures that declaration, and `set_option Elab.async false` at the head of a module elaborates its declarations one after another.  `set_option trace.profiler true`, under `set_option Elab.async false`, names each kernel check above its threshold by the declarations it checks, which locates the costly declaration of a module that finishes.  A probe that a timeout may stop leaves a marker in a file before and after each theorem with `IO.FS.withFile`, as [the entry on measuring Lean checks](../measuring-lean-checks/README.md) describes.

3. Tell the elaborator's work from the kernel's check and from compiled code.  With `set_option debug.skipKernelTC true`, for diagnosis only, the kernel skips the declaration's check, and a declaration that then passes quickly spends its cost in the kernel.  Errors that begin with `(kernel)`, such as `(kernel) deep recursion detected` and `(kernel) deterministic timeout`, come from the kernel, and `deterministic timeout at whnf` or `isDefEq` and `maximum recursion depth has been reached` come from the elaborator.  The kernel's check can stop at the heartbeat limit, as the commands of `tests/verified/TreeFieldConditions.lean` did before the reflector stated record fields over variables, or exhaust memory first, as the comparison that the section comment before `Library.Numerics.Float64.Lgamma.LgammaSign.sgnOf` describes did.  Lean checks theorems asynchronously and keeps a theorem that fails the kernel as an axiom, so read `#print axioms` of a later theorem or list the constants whose `ConstantInfo` is an `axiomInfo` ([the entry on large folds and compiled terms](../large-term-unfolding/README.md)), and `leanchecker`, which comes with the toolchain, replays a module's declarations through the kernel (`docs/trusted-base.md`, section "Shared components").  Compiled code that runs slowly under `#eval`, `lean --run`, or an executable is a separate cost, which [the entry on compiled forms of list specifications](../linear-compiled-folds/README.md) treats.

4. Find the term.  In the elaborator, `simp?` lists the lemmas that a `simp` call used, and the profiler's tactic nodes give the time of each step.  For the kernel, follow [the entry on kernel evaluation costs](../kernel-evaluation-costs/README.md): elaborate with `debug.skipKernelTC`, then check in the kernel, one at a time and under a small heartbeat limit, each step of an `Eq.trans` chain or each argument whose type differs syntactically from its binder's type.  A `MetaM` `#eval` can add a part of a proof term as a theorem with `Lean.addDecl`, and `#print` under `set_option pp.proofs true` shows the term.  A truncated proof must use its last hypothesis, since otherwise the kernel never checks the step that built it, and a replacement of hypotheses by `sorry` misses a cost that lies in a lemma's application, as the bisection of `print64_bound_le` in [the entry on large folds and compiled terms](../large-term-unfolding/README.md) shows.  `trace_state` followed by `done` prints the goals at a point of a proof without closing them.  To locate the element at which a `decide +kernel` on the equality of two long lists fails, state theorems that compare the same range of both sides through `List.take` and `List.drop`, check each alone under `set_option Elab.async false`, and `#print` the definitions in the first range that fails.  This division of `ClightN.Proof.sha_defs_eq` of `clightn/sha/Sha.lean`, the comparison of the SHA-256 instance's definitions with the file's, located the failure in the function `lx_init`, whose stores `LeanExe.Native.initStores` computes and which the kernel cannot evaluate when `initStores` is defined by well-founded recursion ([the entry on definitions that the kernel evaluates](../kernel-evaluated-definitions/README.md)).

5. Find how the cost grows.  Run probes at several values of one size, such as a count of bindings, loop passes, or table rows, or the value of a literal, beside a control at a small value, and read the mechanism from the growth.  A cost linear in the value of a literal points to arithmetic that the kernel performs one successor at a time, as the header of `tests/verified/TreeFieldConditions.lean` states for the literal `2^63`, and a cost that grows by a constant factor when a count doubles points to work that the kernel repeats, as the docstring of `Library.Random.ParkMiller.check_value` states for `LeanExe.loop`.  `set_option diagnostics true` with `set_option diagnostics.threshold N` lists the declarations that the elaborator and the kernel unfolded more than `N` times (`Lean/Meta/Diagnostics.lean` in the toolchain's sources), which names the function under evaluation, although a count of unfoldings does not give the time.

6. Fix the cause and confirm the fix.  Compare the changed check with its baseline on one host under the same limits, in alternating runs, and show that the change kept the statements and the emitted bytes ([the entry on changes that keep statements and bytes](../unchanged-statements-and-bytes/README.md)).  A test that failed before the fix, such as `tests/verified/TreeFieldConditions.lean`, keeps the fix in place.  Raise `maxHeartbeats` or `maxRecDepth` only for the declaration that needs it and only once the cause is known, with a heartbeat limit near twice the use that `#count_heartbeats in` measures under `set_option Elab.async false`, as [the Sail README's section on the proofs](../../../tools/sail/README.md#the-proofs) sets its limits.

| Cause | Signs | Remedy | Details and worked examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| One declaration evaluates too much, and the kernel keeps every reduction until the declaration ends | Memory grows through one check, while its parts check quickly | Check the parts as separate declarations and join them with a lemma, and give a heavy range its own module | [Closed computations](../closed-computations/README.md), [kernel checks over ℚ](../kernel-rational-checks/README.md), [image files](../image-file-checks/README.md) |
| The evaluated definition repeats work that the kernel does not share | The cost grows faster than the input, and one function dominates the unfolding counts | A form that computes each value once as data, proved equal to the form of the proofs, or a native evaluation whose literals the kernel checks | [Definitions that the kernel evaluates](../kernel-evaluated-definitions/README.md), [image files](../image-file-checks/README.md) |
| A comparison of two different terms makes the kernel evaluate one of them | The declaration passes under `debug.skipKernelTC`, and a projection of a large block, a beta redex, a closed sum, a match, or a large literal takes part | Make the compared terms equal: equations over variables applied afterwards, `congrArg` outside a flattening, hints with the kernel's binder types, statements attached to proofs by `rfl`, and a named definition for a projected component | [Kernel evaluation costs](../kernel-evaluation-costs/README.md), [large folds and compiled terms](../large-term-unfolding/README.md) |
| Arithmetic on a variable and a large literal, in the kernel or in unification | Memory grows during a walk, or `rw`, `generalize`, or `omega` reaches the recursion limit | Lemmas over variables, a quantity named by a variable whose equation `LeanExe.A64.Isla.Opaque` hides, `LeanExe.A64.Isla.nat_add_zero`, generalized powers, and `exponentiation.threshold` set for one declaration | [Kernel evaluation costs](../kernel-evaluation-costs/README.md), [instruction forms against Isla](../isla-form-proofs/README.md), [correct rounding](../correct-rounding/README.md), [closed computations](../closed-computations/README.md) |
| `rfl`, `show`, `change`, or `decide` unfold large definitions | Timeouts at `whnf` or `isDefEq` on goals that hold IEEE operations, folds, or loops | `simp only` or `dsimp only` with the definitions, rewrites with equation lemmas, and `with_reducible rfl` | [Large folds and compiled terms](../large-term-unfolding/README.md), [accuracy against real functions](../real-function-accuracy/README.md), [tactics whose failures mislead](../tactic-combinator-pitfalls/README.md) |
| Tactic search that grows with the context or the term | One tactic uses most of the heartbeats | Premises cleared before `tauto`, named nodes of a large literal, splits before unfolding, `omega` given only the facts it needs, `linarith only`, and local definitions replaced by their values | [Tactics whose failures mislead](../tactic-combinator-pitfalls/README.md), [instruction forms against Isla](../isla-form-proofs/README.md), [tail-termination cases](../tail-termination-cases/README.md), [correct rounding](../correct-rounding/README.md) |
| Source forms of a `verified_compile` program | The command runs out of heartbeats or recursion depth | A listed function for a costly condition, helpers inlined or listed according to their parameters, and call results named by `let` | [Source forms and costs](../verified-source-costs/README.md), [byte buffers in words](../byte-buffer-passes/README.md) |
| A WASI action defined by recursion on a fuel, or by well-founded recursion, with a large literal argument | `(kernel) deep recursion detected` at a generated `start` or `total` theorem alone, at a literal that a smaller value avoids | An action constructor that holds the iteration, such as `LeanExe.Wasi.Action.loop`, which unfolds in one step whatever the literal | [Programs that read their whole input](../wasi-read-all/README.md) |
| A definition by well-founded recursion under `decide +kernel` | The kernel's check fails as an application type mismatch although the statement holds, and a comparison divided into ranges fails in the ranges that the definition computes | Structural recursion, which `termination_by structural` requires of the definition | [Definitions that the kernel evaluates](../kernel-evaluated-definitions/README.md) |
| Info trees and declarations kept to the end of a long file | Memory grows through a file without one costly declaration | `without_info` around the costly tactic proofs, one module per form, and `set_option Elab.async false` | [Instruction forms against Isla](../isla-form-proofs/README.md) |
| Memory pressure | The job stays at its `MemoryHigh` with a small share of a processor | More memory, fewer threads, or separate runner calls | [Measuring Lean checks](../measuring-lean-checks/README.md) |
| Slow compiled code | A native replay or a run over test vectors grows quadratically with its input | A `@[csimp]` theorem to a linear form, placed before the definitions that use it | [Compiled forms of list specifications](../linear-compiled-folds/README.md), [transcriptions of standards](../spec-transcription/README.md) |

A limit raised before the cause is known lets the check run longer and leaves the growth of its cost in place.  A raised `maxRecDepth` fits a term whose nesting is as deep as its data, such as the bytes of a file, and it does not help when the depth comes from an evaluation that a comparison forces: the docstring of `Library.Numerics.Float64.Bessel.BesselYnTheorem.yn_eq` records a check that ran for 15 minutes under a raised limit without finishing, and [the entry on large folds and compiled terms](../large-term-unfolding/README.md) records the same for a function defined by well-founded recursion.  Two remedies rest on one case each and are uncertain: a definition that matches its discriminants one at a time, in [the entry on large folds and compiled terms](../large-term-unfolding/README.md), and the effect of a raised `exponentiation.threshold` on unification, in [the entry on closed computations](../closed-computations/README.md).  Some costs remain unexplained, and the entries that record them say so, among them the timeout at `whnf` in [the entry on byte buffers in words](../byte-buffer-passes/README.md) and the kernel's timeout in [the entry on word identities](../word-bit-identities/README.md).

The worked examples are those of the linked entries.  `tests/verified/TreeFieldConditions.lean` shows a cause found by steps 3 to 5 and kept by step 6, `Library.Random.ParkMiller.check_value` shows a growth law that named its cause, and `Library.Numerics.Float64.Lgamma.LgammaSign.sgnOf` and `Library.Numerics.Float64.Bessel.BesselYnTheorem.yn_eq` show the kernel's comparisons that the steps located.
