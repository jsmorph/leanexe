# Counts over nested sets

The entry applies when a proof compares counts of the items of a list under two classifications, such as the votes of the candidates of a set `S` and of a subset `S'`, where each count is a sum, over the members of a set, of the items that the classification assigns to the member.  Member by member the sums do not compare: a member of `S'` can receive more items against `S'` than against `S`, since items move to it from the members that `S'` lacks, and only the total over the set decreases.  The comparison holds item by item, through the properties of the classification.

Rewrite each sum over a set without duplicates as one count of the items that a membership predicate accepts.  `Demos.Rcv.Stability.sum_count_eq_countP` turns the sum of `os.count (.counts i)` over the members `i` of `L` into `os.countP (countsIn L)` when `L` has no duplicates, by induction on `L` with `Demos.Rcv.Stability.countP_or_of_disjoint`, which splits the count of a disjunction of disjoint predicates into a sum, and `Demos.Rcv.Stability.continuing_eq_countP` applies it to the continuing votes of a round.  Then move the classification into the predicate with `List.countP_map`, so that both counts range over the same items, and reduce the inequality to one implication per item with `List.countP_mono_left`.  `Demos.Rcv.Stability.countP_transfer` proves that implication from the classification's properties, `Demos.Rcv.Stability.Closed` and `Demos.Rcv.Stability.Stable`: an item that counts for a member of `L'` against `S'` counts against `S` for a member of `L'` or for a member of `S` outside `S'`.  To separate one member's count from the others', apply `countP_or_of_disjoint` from right to left, as `Demos.Rcv.Stability.majority_subset` writes the continuing votes as the votes of `w` plus the count of the other members, and `Demos.Rcv.Stability.votesFor_le_countP` bounds one member's count by the count of a set that holds it.

The worked examples are `Demos.Rcv.Stability.continuing_mono` and `Demos.Rcv.Stability.tally_follows`, which state that the continuing votes do not increase from a set to a subset, `Demos.Rcv.Stability.two_le_continuing`, `Demos.Rcv.Stability.majority_subset`, and `Demos.Rcv.Batch.BatchAt.batch_lt`, which joins `countP_transfer` with the counts of a batch.  All of them lie in the ranked-choice demos.  [The entry on traces of fuel-bounded runs](../run-traces/README.md) gives the rounds whose sets these lemmas compare.
