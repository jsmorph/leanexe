# Total execution of a WASI process

A WASI process runs `begin` and then a step until the step stops, and its total theorem states that the compiled module exits with the specified output for every input within stated limits.  `Verified.Wasi.compiled_process_total_run` gives that theorem from `ProcessTotalPremises`, a host environment that satisfies `hostSpec host`, a well-founded relation on ghost states, and the facts about the initial heap.  The integer and binary64 running sums prove their final theorems this way, and a new process uses the same method.

The compiled process compacts its state after `begin` and after each step: it copies the state, moves the copy down to the heap's base, and resets the top, so that between steps the heap holds the literal pool and one state.  `Verified.Wasi.ProcessStored` states that arrangement and `wp_process_compact` proves that the compaction produces it.  `ProcessTotalPremises` therefore requires room one step at a time: `beginRoom`, `stepRoom`, and the conditions inside `beginReplies` and `stepReplies` compare the starting top plus a step's charge and the copies of the states before and after it with the memory cap.  `beginBounded` and `stepBounded` are the prefix bounds of [the entry on WASI effect charges](../wasi-effect-charges/README.md).  The state's type must satisfy `Verified.Ty.compactable`, which excludes trees, and `Verified.Ty.copyCost_mod` shows that the copy of such a state costs a multiple of 8 bytes.

Each application states its charges as an intermediate structure under an invariant and derives it from limits on the input.  `Demos.RunningSum.RunCharges` holds a charge for each state, a charge for `begin`, their prefix bounds, and the room conditions, for the states where an invariant holds.  `Demos.RunningSum.limitCharges` builds it from `InputLimits L D` and the inequality `heap.top + limitMemory L D ≤ 65536 · cap`, through `Demos.RunningSum.Sized.copyCost`, `step_sized`, and `limitMemory_room`.  `Demos.RunningSum.initial_terminates_integer` then states the theorem over the limits with that one inequality, and `Demos.RunningSumFp.initial_terminates_fp` does the same for lines of at most `L` bytes.

The order book's process, whose theorem is `Demos.OrderBook.Process.initial_terminates`, keeps the arrays of its state at fixed sizes, which `Demos.OrderBook.Process.Sized` states, so one charge, `Demos.OrderBook.Process.stepCharge R`, bounds every step from a state of those sizes, whatever records the input holds.  Its room condition is then the closed inequality `Demos.OrderBook.Process.initial_room`, which `decide` proves, and `Demos.OrderBook.Process.terminates_of_room` states the theorem for every initial store with that room.

The ranked-choice tabulator's process, whose theorems are `Demos.Rcv.Process.initial_terminates` and `Demos.Rcv.Process.initial_exits_long`, reads its whole input in `begin` with `LeanExe.Wasi.readAll` and tabulates it there, so `begin` carries the largest charge, the buffer and the computation, and the steps only write a report of bounded size.  Its room condition is one closed inequality, `Demos.Rcv.Process.processMemory_le`, and `Demos.Rcv.Process.Compiled.target_terminates` takes the status at which `begin` stops, so the run on an input above the limit, which stops in `begin`, has its own theorem with an invariant of the steps that no state meets.  [The entry on programs that read their whole input](../wasi-read-all/README.md) explains why such a process reads in `begin` rather than in its steps.

Processes that share a state type and their steps, and differ in a pure function and its compilation, share one proof of their runs.  `Demos.Rcv.Process.Compiled` bundles a program of `verified_compile_wasi` whose entry is `Demos.Rcv.Process.processEntry`, which fixes the state's type as `Demos.Rcv.Process.stateTy` by definition, with the facts that the process theorems take: the calls, the runtime, the profile, the literal pool, the closure of the compaction, and the relations of the compiled `begin` and `step` to the native ones.  `Demos.Rcv.Process.Compiled.target_terminates`, `Demos.Rcv.Process.Compiled.terminates_of_room`, and `Demos.Rcv.Process.Compiled.exits_long_of_room` hold for every such bundle, and each process instantiates them with a definition whose fields are its compilation's declarations, `Demos.Rcv.Process.compiledProcess` for Oakland and `Demos.Rcv.Maine.Process.compiledProcess` for Maine.  The process theorems, such as `Demos.Rcv.Maine.Process.initial_terminates`, name the process's own `module` and `startIndex`, which unfold to the bundle's.  A bundle that took any entry with the premise `entry.state = stateTy` would need a cast of `ProcessStep entry.state.denote` in every statement.  The native `begin` is stated once over the pure function, as `Demos.Rcv.Process.beginWith`, which each process's `begin` equals by definition, so the compiler reads a first-order `begin` while `Demos.Rcv.Process.beginWith_report` and `Demos.Rcv.Process.beginWith_long` serve both proofs.  The charges of the bodies, such as `Demos.Rcv.Process.writeStep_bounded`, name the reflected bodies and their function indices, and `Demos.Rcv.Process.compactClosed`, `Demos.Rcv.Process.initialState`, `Demos.Rcv.Process.initial_memoryCap`, and `Demos.Rcv.Process.initial_top` evaluate the concrete module, so each process proves them for its own compilation.

An effect function that a host calls as an export, without a loop of steps, has the generated theorem `p.f.total` of `verified_compile_wasi`, whose conclusion `Verified.Wasi.ReturnOrAcceptedExit` states that the call returns the action's value with its trace or that the action exits and the run ends with the exit trap and the host's exit code.  Its premises are the host's `Satisfies`, the absence of traps along the action, `host.NoTrapsAlong`, the facts of the initial store, and a budget with its prefix bound and room in memory.  `Verified.Examples.WasiRetain.retain_total` applies it with the budget of 112 bytes that `Verified.Examples.WasiRetain.call_prefix_bound` proves by the method of [the entry on WASI effect charges](../wasi-effect-charges/README.md), and with `initial_within`, a closed check of the room.

`Demos.RunningSum.Termination.target_terminates` takes `StepAdvances`, which gives for each step a represented ghost of the final world at which the invariant holds and the rank falls.  The ghost must be the one at which the rank falls, because the host's representation relation is not functional and two replies may give different positions for one world.  The stream lemmas that produce a position therefore also state the fall of the rank at that position.

A budget for a whole run cannot be proved.  The heap model charges every allocation against `top` and credits no release, so the charge of a run grows with every read, and with a host that returns one byte per read no budget below `2^32` bytes covers an input above about one million bytes.  `ProcessTotalPremises` therefore uses the compaction and the room conditions per step.
