# A new flat call on the native target

The entry applies when a WASI command imports a flat call that the Linux shim does not implement, and the command's AArch64 Linux executable must make it.  The executable needs a shim function for every flat import of the command, and a theorem of the form `calls_supported : ∀ call ∈ command.sysCalls, call ∈ shimCalls`, which `decide +kernel` proves or refutes at once, shows a missing call before any plan depends on it, as `Demos.ManifestCheck.calls_supported` does for the manifest checker.  A new call needs parts at every level from the Linux call layer to the documents, and `fd_filestat_get`, which makes one `statx`, gives an example of each.

The Linux call layer must describe each Linux call that the shim makes.  `tools/linux-calls.py` lowers the calls of its list `ADMITTED` from syzlang's descriptions into `LeanExe/Linux/CallTable.lean` and the frame test `tests/linux/sys/frame.c`, and it refuses a call whose lowering it does not support, so the record of `statx`, whose fields include structures and arrays of a fixed count of integers, needs the script's lowering of such fields.  A call joins the list after the review of the kernel's code that [the entry on Linux calls through the call layer](../linux-call-layer/README.md) describes.  The call also needs a step of the frame test and a seeded fault of `FAULTS` in the same script, such as the step of `statx` of an open file with `AT_EMPTY_PATH` and the fault `statx-record-short`, and a place in the list `CALLS` of the second lowering, `tests/linux/syzlang-lowering.py`, as [the entry on checks that can fail](../discriminating-checks/README.md) describes for the frame test.

The shim of `Verified/Shim/` needs the call's reply, `Verified.Shim.filestatReply` in `Verified/Shim/Replies.lean`, its layer event and the lemma that evaluates the layer's step for it, `Verified.Shim.statxEvent` and `Verified.Shim.step_statx` in `Verified/Shim/Memory.lean`, and its image, `Verified.Shim.FilestatImage` in `Verified/Shim/Host.lean`, with a case of `Verified.Shim.SysImage`, the equation `Verified.Shim.sysImage_filestat`, and the call in `Verified.Shim.shimCalls`.  It needs the function and its specification, `Verified.Shim.filestatInvoke` and `Verified.Shim.filestat_contract` in `Verified/Shim/Files.lean`, with a case of `Verified.Shim.sysInvoke` and of `Verified.Shim.sys_contract` in `Verified/Shim/Satisfies.lean`.  Each lemma that splits on the calls of `shimCalls`, such as `Verified.Shim.image_namesHeld`, `Verified.Shim.sysImage_trapped`, and `Verified.Shim.sysImage_calls`, needs a case for the new image.  An output that lies in the shim's scratch rather than in guest memory, as the record of `statx` does, also needs a field of `Verified.Shim.ShimLayout`, here `Verified.Shim.ShimLayout.statx`, a disjunct of `Verified.Shim.WritesWithin`, and the agreement of two layouts on the field in `Verified.Shim.Exec.streams` and `Verified.Shim.Prefix.streams`, which [the entry on WASI hosts built on a lower layer](../wasi-host-from-layer/README.md) describes.

The A64 binding needs the stub, `LeanExe.A64.filestatStub` in `LeanExe/A64/WasiFiles.lean`, among `LeanExe.A64.fileStubs` in `LeanExe/A64/Wasi.lean`, and, for a stub that reads the shim's descriptors, the import's name in `LeanExe.A64.preopenNames` of `LeanExe/A64/WasiPreopen.lean`, whose names make the startup check descriptor 3.  The stub's run lemmas, such as `LeanExe.A64.run_filestatFront` and `LeanExe.A64.run_filestatBack` in `LeanExe/A64/Proof/WasiFilestat.lean`, follow its code in the program's model, `Verified.ShimA64.filestatStub_run` and `Verified.ShimA64.filestatStub_sim` in `Verified/ShimA64/Filestat.lean` prove `LeanExe.A64.Machine.StubSim` on the shim's machine, and `Verified.ShimA64.fileStub_sim` adds the stub to the case of the file stubs in `Verified.ShimA64.flatStub_sim`.  An output in the scratch also needs its place in `LeanExe.A64.Scratch`, here `LeanExe.A64.Scratch.statx`, and a case of `Verified.ShimA64.WritesNative`, which admits that output region outside linear memory.  [The entry on simulations over a machine](../simulation-over-a-machine/README.md) describes the proofs of the stubs of imports.

The documents need the call's rows in the tables of [the WASI manual's section on the Linux shim's specification](../../../docs/wasi.md#the-linux-shims-specification) and of its section [Differences from Wasmtime](../../../docs/wasi.md#differences-from-wasmtime), and the rows "The kernel's agreement with the frame rule" and "The shim's specification" of [the trusted base's section on AArch64 Linux executables](../../../docs/trusted-base.md#a64l-aarch64-linux-executables) need the call and its evidence.  The worked example is `fd_filestat_get` in the files above, with `Demos.ManifestCheck.calls_supported` and `Demos.ManifestCheck.stat_native`, which gives the image of the manifest checker's `fd_filestat_get` under the shim.
