Shuffle an array of UInt64 words in place with Algorithm P of TAOCP section 3.4.2.  The caller
supplies the random numbers as an array of words, and each word u serves as the fraction u / 2^64
in Knuth's step P3.  The result must be a permutation of the input for every word array, and when
the words are independent and uniform, every arrangement of distinct words must be equally likely
up to the rounding of 64-bit fractions.
