Multiple-precision arithmetic on nonnegative integers, Algorithms A, S, and M of TAOCP section
4.3.1.  A number is an array of UInt64 words, each a digit below 2^32 in base b = 2^32, least
significant digit first.  Provide three functions of two numbers u and v, with m digits and n
digits, and let k be the larger of m and n.  Addition returns the k + 1 digits of u + v.
Subtraction returns the k digits of u - v when u >= v, and of u - v + b^k when u < v.
Multiplication returns the m + n digits of u * v.
