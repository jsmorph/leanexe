do Int64 decimal reader and printer

The session that ran the request stated it in these terms.  A printer from Int64 to its canonical
decimal text: "-" for negatives, no leading zeros, "0" for zero, and "-9223372036854775808" for the
minimum.  A reader from text to Int64 that accepts exactly a documented grammar, rejects everything
else (empty input, stray bytes, and values outside [-2^63, 2^63 - 1]), and round-trips with the
printer.  The specification settles the grammar's open choices (a leading "+", leading zeros, and
"-0") with reasons and says how the result reports rejection.  Text uses the byte representation
that the library already uses for text in compiled programs.
