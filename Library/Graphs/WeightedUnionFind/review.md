# Review of the specification

Two fresh agents reviewed the specification, `LeastLabels` of [the module of Algorithm E](../UnionFind/Spec.lean), and the case inputs against [the request](request.txt), from the files alone, each with an independent Python model of the program at 64-bit words and an oracle of the request.  Both found that `LeastLabels` states the request's result on every input, with pairs past the shorter array, elements of `n` or more, and `n` of 0 and 1, and their models agreed with their oracles on all 678 cases and on 6,000 and 4,000 further inputs.  The first checked at every pair that both paths end with their roots as parents, that the sizes at the roots count the elements of their trees, and that the smaller tree goes under the larger with ties as the docstring states, and it found every tree at most `⌊lg n⌋` high.  The second checked the claims of the proof, among them `compress_spec` for every count of steps, `link_inv` in both directions, and `labels_spec` on arbitrary forests that satisfy `Inv`.

Both found that the comment of the shared specification described the module of Algorithm E: three arrays of `n` words, "in place" as the update of the parent links alone, and Algorithm E as the method that the specification does not distinguish.  This module now has [its own specification file](Spec.lean), whose comment names the four arrays, the update of the parent links and the sizes in place, and the method's parts that no statement contains.  Both found that the docstring of `compress` said that every element on the path gets the root as parent, which holds when the count of steps covers the path, and that the docstring of `unite` named the smaller tree where the code compares the recorded sizes, which no theorem relates to the trees.  Both docstrings now say so, the compilation's comment says that no theorem joins `classes_call` and `classes_spec`, and the proof's comments now state `rootOf_eq_of` and the origin of the labeling loop exactly.

Both ran wrong programs and found that the cases detect every program that changes a class, while no case can detect an error of compression, of the direction of a link, or of the sizes, since `labels_spec` fixes the result for any links that satisfy `Inv`.  Both found that the inputs of Algorithm E build trees of depth 1 under union by size, that no case searched more than 4 links, and that the docstring of those inputs describes long paths that this program does not build.  The cases now add binomial trees of 32 and 4,096 elements, in which searches of 5, 11, and 12 links compress their paths and a pair joins two trees through two long paths, and a small forest whose search compresses a path.
