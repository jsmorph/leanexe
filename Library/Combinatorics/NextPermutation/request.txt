Given an array of UInt64 words, compute the next arrangement of its words in lexicographic order
with one step of Algorithm L of TAOCP section 7.2.1.2: return the least arrangement of the same
words, repetitions included, that is greater than the input, with the flag 1.  When the input is
the greatest arrangement, return the least arrangement, the words in nondecreasing order, with the
flag 0, so that repeated calls cycle through the arrangements.  Work in place, with a call stack
that does not grow.
