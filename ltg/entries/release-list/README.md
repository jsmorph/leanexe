# Releasing a list or tree of records

Use this entry when a hint has rule `release-list`.  The compiler translates a fold over a list that a call returns, such as `(listRange n).foldl (· + ·) 0`, to the call into a fresh local, the fold of the `list-fold-loop` entry over that local, and `Stmt.release src` on it after the fold, with hint `release-list`.  `Stmt.releaseNode_spec` proves the release from a precondition that fixes the store and the state.  Its premises are `Heap.At`, `NodeOwned` for the value, and the pairwise disjointness of its blocks; `Represent.owned` gives the last two for the call's result, and `NodeOwned.listAt` gives the fold its `ListAt` premise from the same fact.

The postcondition keeps the state and gives some heap with the allocator invariant, the same memory caps, and a frame clause: every region of positive size of the heap before the release that lies apart from the value's blocks keeps its bytes and stays a region.  The call's region clause leaves every region of the caller's heap apart from the result's blocks, so the release keeps those regions too, and composing the two gives the region clause of `Implements`.  `Project.Lists.sumRange_implements` is the worked example: 57 lines for a call, a fold, and a release.

`release_tree_run` proves the runtime's `release` on any tree of records.  Its loop invariant `Releasing` describes the pending records, linked through their count words, each with its subtree owned; the measure is `forestRecords`, a ghost index that `wp_loop_ghost` takes in place of a function of the store.  `Releasing.next` proves one iteration on the model: the head record's children, linked by `dropChildren` in slot order, come first in reverse order, and the head's block joins the free list.  `dropChildren_spec` and `freeObject_spec` connect the model to the code, and both hold for any locals of `release` (`ReleaseVars`).
